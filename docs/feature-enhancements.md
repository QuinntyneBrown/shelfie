# Feature enhancements

Candidate enhancements beyond the current L1 scope, ordered by value add (highest first). Effort: **S** ≤ 1 day, **M** 2–4 days, **L** 1–2 weeks.

| # | Name | Description | Effort |
|---|------|-------------|--------|
| 1 | AI-assisted questions | Generate chapter-specific comprehension questions from the book and chapter, which the parent can approve or edit, replacing generic defaults. | L |
| 2 | Book autocomplete | Type a title and pick a match; author, chapter count and cover fill in automatically using a book catalogue with AI fallback. | M |
| 3 | Cover photo picker | After choosing a book from autocomplete, browse a gallery of cover photos found online and pick one as the book's cover. | M |
| 4 | AI review assistant | Flags answers that show weak understanding and drafts an encouraging feedback note the parent can send or edit. | M |
| 5 | Streak rewards | Parent-set rewards unlock at streak milestones (7, 30, 100 days), keeping daily reading motivated between finished books. | M |
| 6 | Achievement badges | Earn badges for feats like two chapters in one day, first book, or a weekend of reading, shown on her shelf. | M |
| 7 | Reward gallery | Parent curates a gallery of rewards; she picks the one she's reading toward and sees progress to it. | M |
| 8 | Voice answers | Dictate answers with speech-to-text so typing never slows her down; she can edit the text before sending. | S |
| 9 | Next book recommendations | AI suggests next books from her finished books and ratings; parent adds one to the shelf in a tap. | M |
| 10 | Book rating | On finishing, she rates the book with stars and an emoji reaction; feeds recommendations and her trophy shelf. | S |
| 11 | Feedback notifications | Push notifications tell the parent a chapter awaits review and tell her when a sticker or reward arrives. | M |
| 12 | Reading goals | Parent sets a weekly chapter goal; a playful progress bar on the shelf shows how close she is. | S |
| 13 | Streak freeze | One missed day per week doesn't break the streak, so a busy day isn't discouraging. | S |
| 14 | Vocabulary log | Save new words from a chapter with a kid-friendly definition; review them later as flashcards. | M |
| 15 | Reply to feedback | She can react to the parent's sticker or note with an emoji, closing the conversation loop. | S |
| 16 | Read-aloud questions | Questions are read aloud with text-to-speech on tap, helping when a word is tricky. | S |
| 17 | Reading stats | Charts of chapters, books and streaks over time for both users, plus a cheerful year-in-review. | M |
| 18 | Barcode scan | Scan a book's barcode with the device camera to add it instantly. | S |
| 19 | Finish certificate | Printable, personalised certificate generated when a book is finished, for the fridge. | S |
| 20 | Weekly parent digest | Weekly e-mail summarising chapters read, answers, streak and pending reviews. Needs the deferred e-mail provider. | M |
