Why Shelfie

Reading that feels like a game, not homework.

Children read more when they can see how far they have come and know someone is cheering them on. Shelfie keeps both in one bright, simple place.

📚

Her shelf, her progress.

Every book shows how many chapters she has read. Finished books move to a shelf of their own.

💬

Questions that build understanding.

A few friendly questions after each chapter help her think about what she read, in her own words.

🎁

You stay in the loop.

Read her answers, send a sticker and a note, and give the reward you promised when she finishes.

How it works

Five small steps, one big finish.

  1. Read a chapter

    She picks a book from her shelf and reads the next chapter, on paper or on screen.

  2. Log it

    One tap on Log Chapter and a choice of today, yesterday or another day.

  3. Answer the questions

    A sentence or two for each question. Spelling doesn’t matter, and she can speak her answers.

  4. A parent reviews

    You read her answers, choose a sticker and add a note. She sees it next time she opens Shelfie.

  5. A reward for finishing the book

    The last chapter sets off a celebration, and the reward you give shows on her trophy shelf.
