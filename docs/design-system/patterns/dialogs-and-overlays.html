Dialogs and overlays

Shelfie has exactly two kinds of overlay: a modal dialog for a short decision in context, and the account menu. Anything longer, or anything that deserves its own address, is a page.

Overview

Overlays interrupt. Shelfie uses them only where the interruption is the point: Dad confirming that a book should be removed, Dad naming a reward the moment a book is finished, Dad adding a question while looking at the book's question list, and either of them opening the account menu.

  • Dialog: modal, one task, one to three fields or one decision. Three of them: give a reward, remove a book, add or edit a question.
  • Menu: a short list of account actions under the avatar. One of them: the account menu (Settings for Dad, Sign out).
  • Dialog: a short task that interrupts nothing else, such as Add a reader or Invite a parent from Settings.
  • Page: everything else, including add a book, log a chapter and review a chapter, which are long enough to scroll and worth returning to by link.

The system has no drawer, popover or tooltip. Under 640px a dialog becomes a bottom sheet; that is the only mobile variant.

When to use

NeedUseShelfie example

CelebrateA page, never a dialog"You finished the whole book!"

Stacking

  • One overlay at a time. A dialog never opens another dialog; a menu closes before anything it opens appears.
  • Toasts (--z-toast) sit above dialogs (--z-modal) and the backdrop (--z-overlay), so a "Sent to Nia" toast is visible as the dialog closes.
  • The menu (--z-dropdown) sits above the sticky top bar (--z-sticky) and below the backdrop.

Structure

A dialog is a backdrop plus a panel with three parts: a header (optional danger icon, title, optional description, close button), a body, and a sticky footer with the safe action first and the main action last. The examples render in flow with .dialog--static.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Dialogs follow the WAI-ARIA APG Dialog (Modal) pattern; remove a book follows the Alert Dialog variant. role="dialog", aria-modal="true", aria-labelledby the title and aria-describedby the description when there is one. Everything behind is inert. The account menu follows the APG Menu Button pattern: aria-haspopup="menu", aria-expanded, role="menu" with role="menuitem" links.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabCycles within the dialog only.
EscCloses the dialog or menu (same as Cancel / Not now) and returns focus to the trigger. Ignored while busy.
Enter in a fieldSubmits a dialog form.
Enter, Space, ↓ on the avatarOpens the menu with the first item focused.
↑ / ↓, Home / EndMove between menu items.
Tab in the menuCloses it and moves on.

Focus

On open, focus goes to the first field (give a reward, edit a question) or the safe button (remove a book). On close, it returns to the button that opened the overlay. When a dialog's action removes its trigger (a removed book), focus goes to the page's <h1> on the page Dad lands on.

Labelling

The close button is icon-only with aria-label="Close". The menu is labelled with whose it is. Dividers and the name header inside the menu are role="none" and not focusable.

Motion

The backdrop fades in (--duration-base); the sheet slides up and the centred dialog scales in (--duration-slow, --ease-enter); the menu scales in from its top-right corner (--duration-fast). Under reduced motion all appear at once.

Touch

Footer buttons are 44px tall; the close button is 44px square. On phones the sheet's footer is within thumb reach at the bottom of the screen. Tapping the backdrop does the same as Cancel, except while busy.

Content

  • Confirmation titles are questions that name the thing: "Remove “The Lion, the Witch and the Wardrobe”?"
  • Form-dialog titles name the task or the moment: "Add a question", "Edit question", "Matilda is finished! 🏆".
  • The body says what is lost or what happens next, with numbers: "Nia’s 11 logged chapters and answers for this book will be deleted too."
  • Offer the better path when there is one: "If she just finished it, give a reward instead of removing it."
  • Buttons are outcomes: "Keep the book" / "Remove book and answers"; "Not now" / "Give the reward"; "Cancel" / "Add question".
  • Menu items are short verbs or places: "Settings", "Sign out".
