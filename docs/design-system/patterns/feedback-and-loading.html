Pattern · Feedback

Feedback and loading

Say what happened, where it happened, in the smallest way that is still noticed. Content that is on its way shows as a skeleton in its final shape; an action that is on its way shows on the button that started it.

Overview

Shelfie answers every action and every wait. There are six ways to answer, from quietest to loudest:

  1. Inline status: a few words beside the thing that changed ("✓ Saved just now"). See inline message.
  2. Inline alert: a tinted box inside the form or card that the message is about ("The book wasn’t saved"). See alert.
  3. Toast: a short note in the corner about something that just finished, often with one action (Undo, Retry). See toast and notifications.
  4. Banner: a strip under the top bar for a condition that lasts (offline) or news waiting for her.
  5. Dialog: only when the person must decide before going on. See dialogs and overlays.
  6. Celebration: a full card with confetti for Nia's milestones (a chapter logged, a book finished). See celebration.

For waiting there are two tools only: the skeleton for content and the busy button for actions. Shelfie has no page spinner and no standalone spinner component; a determinate progress bar shows reading progress, not loading.

Structure

Skeleton in the final shape

A skeleton copies the layout it stands in for: the same card, the same cover tile, the same rows. The page header and actions that do not depend on the data render straight away. When the data arrives the whole skeleton is swapped at once.

Both themes

Saving…✓ Saved just now
Saving…✓ Saved just now
  • Loading → loaded: swap the whole region at once; never show half real rows and half skeleton.
  • Loading → failed: the region becomes an error empty state with "Try again"; the header stays.
  • Busy → done: the button returns to normal and the result shows where the person is looking (status, toast or the next page).
  • Busy → failed: the button returns to normal, nothing typed is cleared, and the alert or toast explains.

Responsive behaviour

  • Skeletons use the same grid classes as the content (.grid--auto for book cards, .list__item for rows), so they reflow exactly as the real content will.
  • Inline statuses sit in a .cluster--between with the card title and wrap under it on narrow cards.
  • Toasts sit bottom-right from 640px and span the bottom of the screen on phones; banners are full width at every size and stack their action under the text on phones.
  • Celebrations centre their content and keep the primary button large at every width.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Loading regions set aria-busy="true" and an aria-label ("Loading your shelf"); every skeleton piece is aria-hidden. Inline statuses are role="status" (polite). Failed sends use role="alert". Busy buttons are native buttons with aria-busy and disabled. Celebration headings are announced through a live region.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabSkeletons are not focusable; focus skips to live controls such as "Add a book".
Enter / SpaceA busy button ignores presses until the request ends.
Tab into an alertReaches its "Try again" button in reading order.

Focus

Focus stays on the busy button while it works and remains there when it finishes. When a region fails, focus is not moved; the error is announced. When a save opens a new page (a celebration), focus goes to its heading.

Labelling

The loading region's label says what is loading in plain words. Status text always includes words, never only a dot or a tick: "✓ Saved just now", not "✓".

Motion

Skeleton shimmer and the pulsing dot loop on --duration-loop (1400 ms); the busy ring turns at half that. Under prefers-reduced-motion the shimmer stops and loops run once; the busy ring is a status indicator and keeps meaning without motion because the button is also disabled. Confetti is removed entirely.

Touch

Retry and Undo controls are at least 24px tall with room around them; "Try again" in empty states is a full medium button.

Content

  • Progress words end with an ellipsis: "Saving…". Done words are past tense with a time: "Saved just now".
  • Failures say what did not happen, why, and what is safe: "The book wasn’t saved. Shelfie couldn’t reach the server. Everything you typed is still here; try again."
  • Toasts lead with the result ("Chapter 12 saved") and follow with what is next ("5 to go").
  • Celebrations count: "That’s 12 of 17. Only 5 chapters to go."
  • Loading labels name the thing: "Loading questions", "Loading books".
