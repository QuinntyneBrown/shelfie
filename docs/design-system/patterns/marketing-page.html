Pattern · Marketing

Marketing page

The one public page, at www.shelfie.kids. It tells a family what Shelfie is in the order they wonder about it (what, why, how, what it looks like, what else) and ends every path at "Create a parent account" or "Sign in". It is static HTML outside the Angular workspace, so it is built from tokens, not from sh-* components (ADR-0014).

Structure

PartWhat it holds
HeaderSticky, translucent with a blur on a layer behind it. Logo, four anchor links, "Open the app" as a primary pill.
HeroKicker, H1 with the accent on its second half, a lede of at most four lines, a large primary pill and a text link, a trust line; a phone frame with a real screenshot and two stickers.
Value cardsThree .cards, each with a tinted icon tile in a cover pastel, an H3 and one sentence.
Journey stepsAn ordered list; each step numbered "01" to "05" in the accent, an H3 and one sentence, on the sunken surface.
Screenshot showcaseA focusable, labelled region that scrolls sideways with snap points; each screenshot in a figure with a caption.
Bento gridCards in three columns on desktop, two wide; each with an icon tile, an uppercase kicker and an H3.
Final call to actionCentred icon, H2, lede and two pills: primary "Create a parent account", secondary "Sign in".
FooterBrand and tagline, Product and Project columns, copyright with the current year.

Responsive

  • Below --layout-breakpoint-lg (1024px) the anchor links and header CTA give way to a 44px menu button that opens a full-screen menu.
  • Value cards: one column, three from 768px. Journey: one column, two from 768px, five from 1024px. Bento: one, two, then three columns.
  • Nothing scrolls sideways at any width; only the screenshot region scrolls, inside itself.

Motion

Sections rise --space-6 and fade in over --duration-deliberate with --ease-enter as they scroll into view. Under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce everything is shown at once and smooth scrolling is off.

Accessibility

  • Skip link to #main; one H1; every section headed by an H2.
  • Menu button: aria-controls, aria-expanded and a name that says what it will do ("Open navigation" / "Close navigation"). The closed menu is inert; Escape closes it.
  • Decorative icons and stickers are aria-hidden; every screenshot has alt text that says what it shows.

Tokens

No new tokens. The page uses a copy of tokens/tokens.css and the app's font stack; light and dark follow the device. Icon tiles use --color-cover-* with --color-fg-on-cover.
