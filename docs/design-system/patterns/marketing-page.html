    Pattern · Marketing

    Marketing page

    The one public page, at www.shelfie.kids. It says what Shelfie is in as few words as possible, shows the real app on soft tinted cards, and ends every path at "Create a parent account" or "Sign in". It is static HTML outside the Angular workspace, so it is built from tokens, not from sh-* components (ADR-0017).

    Overview

    The page reads like the app feels: big friendly type, pastel washes, real screenshots, very little text. The hero is a large two-tone headline ("Every chapter, rewarded.") over a field of scattered pastel dots. Every section after it has a title of four words or fewer and no kicker or sub-paragraph; the cards carry the detail. "How it works" is three tinted cards with icon tiles, and "Inside the app" is four tinted feature cards with cropped, slightly rotated screenshots. Sections meet with a wavy edge instead of a straight line.

    When to use

    • Only for the public page at www.shelfie.kids (and its menu-open and reduced-motion states). Signed-in screens use the app shell and the navigation pattern.
    • Use tinted cards with icon tiles to explain steps; use feature cards with a screenshot when showing a real screen. Use plain surface cards (the bento grid) for short facts that need no picture.
    • Don't use the wavy edge, two-tone headlines or rotated screenshots inside the app; they are marketing gestures.

    The example uses the app's .card with --card-bg for brevity. The marketing page itself sets washes with .tint-bg-* classes in shelfie.css, because its Content Security Policy forbids inline styles.

    States

    • Default (default): sections rise into view as they scroll in.
    • Menu open (menu-open): below 1024px the menu button opens a full-screen menu of the anchor links and both calls to action; the rest of the page is inert.
    • Reduced motion (reduced-motion): everything shows at once, nothing rises, smooth scrolling is off.
    • Both themes follow the device. Dark uses the deep tint bases, so the washes stay coloured without glowing.

    Responsive

    • Below --layout-breakpoint-lg (1024px) the anchor links and header CTA give way to a 44px menu button that opens a full-screen menu.
    • The hero headline scales with the viewport from --font-size-5xl to 4.75rem; the phone frame drops under the copy on phones.
    • Value cards: one column, three from 768px. How it works: one column, three from 768px. Inside the app: one, two from 768px, four from 1024px. Bento: one, two, then three columns.
    • Nothing scrolls sideways at any width; the rotated screenshots are clipped by their 14rem window.

    Motion

    Sections rise --space-6 and fade in over --duration-deliberate with --ease-enter as they scroll into view. Under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce everything is shown at once and smooth scrolling is off. The dots, waves and screenshot tilt are static.

    Accessibility

    • Skip link to #main; one H1; every section headed by an H2. The accent half of a headline is an <em> inside the heading, so it is read as one sentence.
    • Menu button: aria-controls, aria-expanded and a name that says what it will do ("Open navigation" / "Close navigation"). The closed menu is inert; Escape closes it.
    • Decorative icons, stickers, dots and waves are aria-hidden or pure CSS; every screenshot has alt text that says what it shows.
    • Text on every wash passes AA in both themes: muted text, accent text and the primary pill are checked against each --color-tint-* (see Color).

    Content

    • Section titles are four words or fewer, two-tone, ending in a full stop: "Fun, not homework.", "Three steps, one finish.", "For the whole family.", "Start her next chapter."
    • No kickers above titles and no sub-paragraphs under them. If a section needs explaining, the cards do it.
    • Cards: an H3 of a few words and at most one short sentence.
    • Calls to action are verbs: "Start reading free", "Create a parent account", "Sign in".

    Do and don't

    Tokens

    The page uses a copy of tokens/tokens.css and the app's font stack; light and dark follow the device. Washes read --color-tint-* through a local --tint variable; icon tiles and dots use --color-cover-* with --color-fg-on-cover; cards and screenshot windows use --radius-xl and --radius-lg; screenshots carry --shadow-3. No marketing-only tokens.
