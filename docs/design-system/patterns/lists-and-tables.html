Pattern · Data display

Lists and tables

Shelfie's collections are small and personal: one family's books and one child's chapters. They are shown whole, newest or next first, with no paging, sorting, filtering or selection.

Overview

Six collections appear in the mocks, each with the lightest structure that does its job:

  • Chapter list (chapter row): every chapter of one book in order, done, next and future. Nia's main way into her answers.
  • Book cards (book card in .grid--auto): the books on her shelf, "On the go" and "Up next".
  • Trophy list (trophy): finished books and their rewards.
  • Book list (list): Dad's Books page; one row per book with status badge and "Manage".
  • Chapters-logged table (table): on Manage book, the only real table; chapter, date, first answer, review status.
  • Review queue and activity feed (list, activity feed): what is waiting for Dad, and what happened this week.

When to use

UseWhenShelfie example
Card gridFew items, each with a picture and one action, browsed by a child.Shelf: On the go, Up next
ListItems with a title, one line of detail, a status and one action. Reads on a phone without scrolling sideways.Books page, review queue, questions on Manage book
Chapter listAn ordered sequence where position matters and each item has a state.Book page
TableSeveral facts per item that Dad compares down a column.Manage book → Chapters logged
FeedEvents in time order, read and not acted on.Review → This week

Long lists

  • No pagination below 50 books. A family's shelf will not reach it; every list is shown whole.
  • Long books fold their future. When a book has many unread chapters (The Wild Robot, 80 chapters), the chapter list shows everything up to the next chapter plus a few, then folds the rest into one flush accordion: "Chapters 11 to 80 · 70 more".
  • Finished books stay on one list, newest first, with titles truncated to one line and the full title in title.
  • A long review queue keeps one list, newest first, with a count badge in the heading and a "Newest first" caption.
  • No sorting, filtering, search, bulk selection or infinite scroll. If a list ever needs them it needs a new design and an estimate first.

Responsive behaviour

  • Book cards fill .grid--auto: one column on phones, as many 18rem columns as fit above that.
  • Lists keep one row per item at every width; the title and meta truncate or wrap inside .list__body, while the badge and button keep their size.
  • The table scrolls sideways inside its wrap on phones rather than turning into cards; the "What happened" column truncates at 20rem. It is the only place in Shelfie that scrolls sideways.
  • The activity feed sits in the right column (.span-4) from 1024px and moves under the queue below that.
  • Chapter rows are at least 44px tall at every width; the status text stays on the right.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Lists are <ul>. The chapter list is a run of links and plain rows inside a card; each done or next row is one <a>, so a screen reader hears one link per chapter. The table is a native <table> with a visually hidden <caption> and scope="col" headers. The long-list fold is a native <details>/<summary>.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves through chapter links, row buttons and table links in order; future chapters are skipped.
EnterOpens the chapter, the log form or Manage book.
Enter / Space on the foldOpens or closes "Chapters 11 to 80".

Focus

The ring draws around the whole chapter row. Opening the fold keeps focus on its summary.

Labelling

Chapter numbers and ticks are aria-hidden; the title says "Chapter 9" and the status says "Dad read it" or "Waiting for Dad" in words. Repeated "Manage" and "Open" links carry the item in visually hidden text or aria-label, so they make sense out of context. Covers and stickers are decorative.

Contrast

Row meta on surface
Next row text on accent tint
Next number on accent
Done tick on success tint
Future chapter on surface
Table header on sunken

Motion

Row hover tints over --duration-fast; the fold's plus sign rotates over --duration-base. Reduced motion removes both.

Touch

Chapter rows and accordion summaries are at least 44px tall and full width. Row buttons are small (36px) and sit alone at the end of their row, away from other targets.

Content

  • Chapters are numbered, not titled. The meta line is the date read and the first line of her first answer.
  • Order says what matters: chapters in reading order; books by status; queue, table, feed and finished books newest first.
  • Statuses are short: "Reading", "Reward to give", "Not started", "Rewarded"; "To review", "Reviewed"; "Dad read it", "Waiting for Dad".
  • Feed lines are sentences with a subject: "Nia logged Chapter 10", "You gave Chapter 9 a Star". Dad is "You" on his own pages.
  • Fold labels give the range and the count: "Chapters 11 to 80 · 70 more".
