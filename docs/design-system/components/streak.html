Streak

A warm pill with a flame and a number: how many days in a row Nia has logged at least one chapter. It sits in her top bar on every reader screen, a small daily reason to read.

Overview

The streak is derived, never edited: it counts consecutive days with at least one chapter logged. Nia sees it in the top bar ("5 days") and in her shelf greeting ("🔥 5-day streak"); Dad sees the same number as a stat on Review. Use the pill only for the streak. Other statuses use a badge.

Anatomy

  1. Flame icon16px (.icon--sm), currentColor, aria-hidden.
  2. Count"5 days" in --text-label, plus visually hidden "reading streak" so it reads as a sentence.
  3. Pill32px tall, 12px side padding, --radius-full, warning tint.

Variants

One variant. In running text the streak is written out instead of drawn as a pill.

🔥 5-day streak · 2 books on the go · 1 reward waiting

Sizes

One size, 32px tall (--control-height-sm), to sit beside the 32px top-bar avatar.

States

The pill is not interactive: no hover, focus, active or disabled. What changes is the number.

StateExampleWhen
Default 5 days reading streakNia has logged a chapter on each of the last five days.
While the page loads 5 days reading streakUnchanged. The top bar comes with the session, so the streak shows even while the shelf below is still skeletons.

Responsive behaviour

  • The pill stays in the top bar at every width, between the spacer and the avatar.
  • It never wraps or truncates; "5 days" is short at any count a child will reach.

Theming

Reads --color-warning-bg and --color-warning-fg: a warm amber that reads as fire without meaning "warning" here. Both tokens flip in dark mode, keeping the pair above 4.5:1.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A plain <span> of text; no role and no ARIA. The flame is aria-hidden.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabSkips the pill; it is not interactive.

Focus

Never focusable.

Labelling

Screen readers read "5 days reading streak" from the visible count and the visually hidden words. Do not put aria-label on the span: a generic element's label is ignored by most screen readers.

Contrast

Count on pill
Count on pill

Motion

None. The streak does not animate when it grows; the celebration screen after logging is where delight happens.

Touch

Not a target. It sits 16px from the avatar so it is never tapped by mistake.

Content

  • Pill: the number and "days" ("5 days"); "1 day" for one.
  • In sentences: "🔥 5-day streak".
  • Dad's stat uses the label "Streak" with "🔥 5" as the value.
