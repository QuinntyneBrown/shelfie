Top bar

The bar across the top of every signed-in page. For Nia it is just the brand, her streak and her avatar. For Dad it adds Review, Books and Settings from 768px.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableNative <header> + <nav>

Overview

Every signed-in page starts with a .topbar. It keeps the way home and the account menu one tap away, and it stays put while the page scrolls. Sign-in, forgot and reset password pages have no top bar; they use the auth layout.

  • Reader: brand (back to her shelf), streak, avatar. Nia has one place to be, so there is no navigation.
  • Grown-up: brand (back to Review), .topbar__nav with Review, Books and Settings, avatar. Below 768px the nav hides and the tab bar takes over.
  • The avatar is a button.avatar that opens the account menu.

Anatomy

  1. Brand.topbar__brand: book icon and "Shelfie" in --text-h4; a link to the home of whoever is signed in.
  2. Nav (grown-up)nav.topbar__nav of .nav-links; the current page has aria-current="page". Review carries a .badge--count. Hidden below 768px.
  3. Spacer.topbar__spacer pushes the rest to the right edge.
  4. Right sideThe reader's .streak pill, then button.avatar (small, rose for Nia, sky for Dad).

Sizes

The bar is always --layout-topbar-height (56px) tall; only its contents change with width.

States

The bar has no state of its own; its links and avatar do. Hover and focus are forced with data-state; current uses the real aria-current="page".

Both themes

Shelfie 5 days
Shelfie 5 days
  • Current nav link: --color-accent-subtle fill and --color-fg-accent text, plus aria-current="page" for screen readers.
  • Avatar open: aria-expanded="true" while the account menu is showing; the hover ring stays so the trigger is visibly tied to the menu.
  • Count badge disappears when nothing is waiting (Review, empty); it never shows "0".

Responsive behaviour

  • Base (phones): brand, spacer, streak or nothing, avatar. Side padding is --layout-margin, so the bar lines up with page content at every width.
  • From --layout-breakpoint-md (768px) the grown-up .topbar__nav appears (display: flex) and the tab bar hides. The two never show together.
  • The bar is position: sticky; top: 0 at --z-sticky. scroll-padding-top on html equals its height plus --space-4, so in-page links and focused fields are never hidden under it.
  • Nothing truncates: the streak pill is short ("5 days") and the avatar shows initials only.

Theming

The bar is --color-bg-surface with a hairline --color-border-default bottom edge, so it separates from the canvas in both themes without a shadow. Nav links read --color-fg-muted, --color-bg-subtle and the accent pair; the streak reads the warning pair; avatars read the cover palette with --color-fg-on-cover. The dark theme changes only those semantic tokens.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A native <header> (the page's banner landmark) holding a native <nav aria-label="Primary"> of links. Current page is aria-current="page". The avatar is a Menu Button (APG): aria-haspopup="menu" and aria-expanded.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabSkip link, brand, nav links (grown-up, 768px and up), avatar, then the page.
EnterFollows the brand or a nav link.
Enter, Space, ↓ on the avatarOpens the account menu and moves focus to its first item.
Esc in the menuCloses the menu and returns focus to the avatar.

Focus

The shared 2px --color-focus-ring with 2px offset on every link and the avatar. The bar never covers focus: scroll-padding-top reserves its height (WCAG 2.4.11).

Labelling

The streak pill has aria-label="5-day reading streak" because "5 days" alone is ambiguous. The avatar says whose menu it opens: "Account menu for Nia", "Account menu for Dad". The Review count badge has aria-label="2 chapters to review". The brand's icon is aria-hidden; the word "Shelfie" names the link.

Motion

Nav links fade their background and colour over --duration-fast. Nothing else moves; the bar does not hide on scroll. Reduced motion zeroes the fade.

Touch

Nav links are --target-comfortable (44px) tall. The small avatar is 32px (--size-avatar-sm), above the 24px minimum, with the spacer keeping it well clear of other targets.

Content

  • The brand is always "Shelfie"; no page titles in the bar (the page header has the <h1>).
  • Grown-up nav labels are one noun each, in this order: Review, Books, Settings. The order never changes between pages.
  • The streak is "N days" (or "1 day"); the avatar shows initials: "N" for Nia, "MB" for Dad.
