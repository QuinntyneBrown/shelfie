Component · Identity

Avatar

A circle that stands for a person: their own photo when they have one, otherwise their initial. Nia is "N" in rose, Dad is "D" in sky, everywhere: the top bar, the member rows in Settings and the accept-invite page.

Overview

Use an avatar wherever a screen is about one person in the household. Each person has an initial and a colour, set once, and may add their own photo, so a nine-year-old can tell at a glance whose screen she is on. The photo covers the initial; the initial and colour stay underneath and show while the photo loads, if it fails, and when it is removed.

  • Static (span.avatar): decoration next to a name, as in the member rows in Settings and on the accept-invite page.
  • Button (button.avatar): the top-bar trigger for the account menu.
  • The colour is chosen from the swatches in the add/edit-reader dialog.
  • The photo is chosen by the person themselves in the photo picker.
  • In Angular, use <sh-avatar>; it renders both forms of content and drops a photo that fails to load.

Avatars are not for books; books use a cover. Do not use them for status; use a badge.

Anatomy

  1. InitialsOne capital in --text-label, dark --color-fg-on-cover on a person colour.
  2. Photo (optional)img.avatar__img over the initial, cropped to the circle with object-fit: cover, alt="".
  3. Circle--radius-full, sized by --avatar-size (32, 40 or 56px), filled with the person's colour.

Variants

The colour is the person. Nia is always rose, Dad always sky. Seven colours exist: rose, sky, sun, mint, lilac, sage and peach. The plain avatar (accent tint) is the fallback before a colour is set.

Button avatar

The top-bar trigger. button.avatar resets the border and adds a hover ring; no inline style needed.
HTML
<button class="avatar avatar--sm avatar--sky" type="button"
        aria-label="Account menu for Dad" aria-haspopup="menu" aria-expanded="false">D</button>

Sizes

Small sits in the 64px top bar. Medium is the default and sits in the member rows in Settings. Large heads the accept-invite page, where the avatar confirms who is joining, and sits in Settings → Your account. Extra large is the photo picker preview.

Small · 32px, medium · 40px, large · 56px, extra large · 64px: --size-avatar-sm/md/lg/xl. Extra large is the preview in the photo picker.

  • Loading shows the initial on the person's colour; the photo fades in over it (--duration-base, none under reduced motion). No skeleton, no layout shift: the circle is the same size either way.
  • Broken: if the photo fails to load, sh-avatar removes the image and the initial stays. A broken-image icon is never shown.
  • Uploading (aria-busy="true", photo picker only) dims the photo and spins a ring over it.
  • Hover draws a 2px ring in --color-border-strong around the circle.
  • Focus uses the shared focus ring, outside the hover ring.
  • There is no disabled avatar: the account menu is always available.

Responsive behaviour

  • Sizes do not change with the viewport. The top-bar avatar is 32px at every width.
  • The avatar never shrinks inside a flex row (flex: none), so a long book title wraps around it instead.

Theming

Person colours reuse the cover pastels: --color-cover-rose, -sky, -sun, -mint, -lilac, -sage and -peach, all with dark --color-fg-on-cover initials in either theme, so Nia's avatar looks the same in light and dark. The plain fallback uses --color-accent-subtle with --color-fg-accent and does follow the theme. A new person colour is a new .avatar--{cover} modifier that sets the same two properties.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Static avatars are a <span aria-hidden="true"> next to the visible name; the photo inside is <img alt=""> for the same reason. Where an avatar stands alone as content (the photo picker preview) it is role="img" with a label such as "Your photo" or "No photo yet". The account trigger is a native <button> following the APG menu button pattern: aria-haspopup="menu" and aria-expanded.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabFocuses the button avatar (last item in the top bar).
Enter / Space / ↓Opens the account menu and moves focus to its first item.
EscCloses the menu and returns focus to the avatar.

Focus

The shared 2px ring with a 2px offset on :focus-visible. When the menu closes, focus comes back here.

Labelling

Initials are not a name a screen reader can use. The button carries aria-label="Account menu for Nia"; a static avatar is hidden because the name is printed beside it ("Nia · Reader").

Motion

The photo fades in once over --duration-base; reduced motion removes the fade. The hover ring appears instantly.

Touch

The 32px top-bar button clears the 24px minimum and has 16px of space on each side in the top bar. Use medium or larger wherever the avatar is the only target on a phone.

Content

  • Initials: the first letter of the display name ("N" for Nia, "D" for Dad).
  • One letter, capital.
  • A photo is the person's own choice. Never show a photo of one person on another's avatar, and never fetch one from outside Shelfie.
  • The button's label says what it opens and for whom: "Account menu for Dad".
