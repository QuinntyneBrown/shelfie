Combobox

A text field that looks things up as she types and drops the matches down as a list she can pick from with the keyboard or a tap. On Add a book it finds the title and fills in the author, the cover picture and, for a suggested match, the chapter count.

Overview

Use the combobox where typing a few letters can save filling in the rest of a form. Shelfie uses it once: the Title field on Add a book. The field is still a plain text field first: whatever she types is the title, with or without a pick, and the form keeps working when the lookup finds nothing or cannot be reached.

The list opens after three characters and a short pause, holds at most five rows, and closes on Escape, on a pick, or when focus leaves. Rows from the book catalogue carry a small book cover; rows from the AI helper carry a badge that says they are guesses. A menu is for actions, a radio group for a few known options; a combobox is for free text with help.

Anatomy

  1. Label and inputA form field with input.input carrying role="combobox", aria-autocomplete="list", aria-expanded and aria-controls.
  2. Panel.combobox__panel: a raised surface under the input, full width, --shadow-3, on the dropdown layer.
  3. Listul.combobox__list with role="listbox", named "Matching books".
  4. Optionli.combobox__option with role="option": a small cover (or the 📖 placeholder), a title, a detail line, and a Suggested badge for AI rows. The active row has aria-selected="true".
  5. Note.combobox__note: searching, no matches, lookup failed, or the "these are guesses" line above suggested rows.

Sizes

The input takes the text field's sizes; Add a book uses .input--lg for the title. The panel has one size: it is as wide as the input, rows are at least --target-comfortable tall, and five rows fit without scrolling. Shelfie never shows more than five.

Lookup failed

Shelfie couldn’t look that up. You can still fill it in yourself.

  • Closed below three characters, after Escape, after a pick, and when focus leaves. The input is an ordinary text field.
  • Searching while the lookup runs, from about 300 ms after the last keystroke. Typing again cancels it. The note has aria-busy="true" and a spinner.
  • Open with one to five rows. The active row has aria-selected="true" and the focus ring; the input keeps focus and names it through aria-activedescendant.
  • Row hover tints the row; a click picks it.
  • No matches and lookup failed are notes in a polite live region. They go away on the next keystroke.
  • Invalid is the text field's own state (aria-invalid, error line) and never comes from the lookup.

Responsive behaviour

  • The panel is always as wide as the input and never wider than the viewport; at 360 px it spans the card.
  • Rows wrap: the title and detail wrap onto new lines, and the Suggested badge drops under them when there is no room beside them. Nothing scrolls sideways and nothing truncates.
  • On phones the panel overlays the next field; it is dismissed by picking, by Escape or by tapping outside.

Only role tokens are used: the panel is --color-bg-surface-raised, the active row --color-accent-subtle, notes --color-fg-muted.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

The WAI-ARIA combobox pattern with list autocomplete and manual selection: the input has role="combobox", aria-autocomplete="list", aria-expanded, aria-controls pointing at the listbox and aria-activedescendant pointing at the active option. Focus never leaves the input. The listbox is named "Matching books"; each option's name is its title and detail. Browser autofill is off (autocomplete="off").

Keyboard

KeyAction
TypingEdits the title; after three characters and a pause the list opens with the first row active.
↓ / ↑Moves the active row, wrapping at the ends. ↓ on a closed list with matches reopens it.
EnterPicks the active row when the list is open; otherwise submits the form as usual.
EscapeCloses the list and keeps what was typed.
TabCloses the list and moves on without picking.

Announcements

A visually hidden polite status says "3 matches. Use the up and down arrows to pick one, or keep typing." when the list opens, and "3 suggestions…" for AI rows. Searching, no matches and lookup failed are read from the note. Picking is announced by the fields it fills (the author field value changes) and by the status "Filled in from the match. Check the chapters."

Contrast and targets

Row text is --color-fg-default and --color-fg-muted on the raised surface; the active row's ring is --color-focus-ring. Rows are at least 44 px tall and span the panel.

Motion

The panel scales in over --duration-fast; under reduced motion it appears at once.

Content

  • Rows are plain text: the title as the catalogue or the helper gave it, trimmed to 120 characters; the detail is "Author" or "Author · N chapters"; "Unknown author" when there is none.
  • Badge: "Suggested". Note above suggested rows: "Not in the book catalogue, so these are guesses. Check them before you add the book."
  • Notes: "Looking that up…" · "No matches. Keep typing, or fill the rest in yourself." · "Shelfie couldn’t look that up. You can still fill it in yourself."
  • Never a technical reason (timeout, 429, "AI"); the helper is "Shelfie" to her.

Code

<div class="field combobox">
  <label class="field__label" for="title">Title</label>
  <input class="input input--lg" id="title" type="text" value="Winn" role="combobox" aria-autocomplete="list"
         aria-expanded="true" aria-controls="title-matches" aria-activedescendant="title-match-1" autocomplete="off">
  <div class="combobox__panel">
    <ul class="combobox__list" id="title-matches" role="listbox" aria-label="Matching books">
      <li class="combobox__option" id="title-match-1" role="option" aria-selected="true">
        <span class="cover cover--sm cover--sage" aria-hidden="true">📖<img class="cover__img" src="…" alt=""></span>
        <span class="combobox__text"><span class="combobox__title">Because of Winn-Dixie</span><span class="combobox__detail">Kate DiCamillo</span></span>
      </li>
    </ul>
  </div>
  <p class="visually-hidden" role="status">1 match. Use the up and down arrows to pick one, or keep typing.</p>
</div>
