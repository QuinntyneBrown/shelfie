Textarea

A multi-line box for words that need room: Nia's answers about a chapter, Dad's note back to her, a question he adds to a book.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableNative <textarea>

Overview

Put .textarea on a native <textarea> inside a form field. Use it whenever a good answer is a sentence or more. For a few words (a title, a reward) use a text field.

It appears in three places:

  • Chapter answers on Log a chapter: one per question, three rows each. This is the heart of the product.
  • Notes from Dad: "Say something to Nia" when reviewing a chapter (four rows) and "A note for Nia" with a reward (two rows).
  • A question in the Add a question dialog (two rows).

Anatomy

  1. Question number (answers only).qa__num in --color-fg-accent, inside the label.
  2. LabelThe question itself, or a short noun phrase for notes.
  3. Text--text-body with --line-height-relaxed for easier reading back.
  4. Resize handleVertical only (resize: vertical); the box never gets wider than its field.

Variants

One look. What changes is the label and the number of rows.

HTML
<div class="field">
  <label class="field__label" for="q3"><span class="qa__num">3.</span> What do you think will happen next?</label>
  <textarea class="textarea" id="q3" name="q3" rows="3"></textarea>
</div>

Sizes

There is no size modifier. Height comes from rows, with a floor of 2.5 × --control-height-md (110px) so even a two-row box is an easy target.

rows="2" for notes and questions, rows="3" for answers, rows="4" for the review note.

States

Same states and tokens as the text field. Hover and focus are forced with data-state.

Answer

Both themes

Write a sentence or two about what happened. There are no wrong answers!
Write a sentence or two about what happened. There are no wrong answers!
  • Read-only is the submitting state: when Nia presses "I’m done!" her answers stay on screen, unchangeable, until the save finishes.
  • Invalid only ever applies to question 1 on Log a chapter and to a too-short question in Add a question.
  • Disabled is not used by the mocks for textareas; it is shown for completeness of the shared field styles.

Responsive behaviour

  • Full width of the field at every size; on the Log a chapter card that is the whole column.
  • Rows stay the same on phones and desktops. Nia can drag the box taller; it never scrolls sideways.
  • Long words and pasted links wrap inside the box, and later inside .qa__a where the answer is shown back.

Theming

Shares --field-bg and --field-border with .input, so every state and both themes match the text field exactly. The only textarea-specific values are the minimum height and --line-height-relaxed.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Native <textarea> (a multi-line textbox), labelled by <label for>. Help and error join through aria-describedby, error first.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves to the next or previous answer. Tab never inserts a tab character.
EnterInserts a new line. It does not submit the form, so Nia cannot send half an answer by accident.

Focus

Focus border plus the shared ring with no offset, as on the text field. In the Add a question dialog focus starts in the textarea.

Labelling

On Log a chapter the label is the full question with its number, so a screen reader announces "1. What happened in this chapter?". The fieldset's legend ("Tell Dad about it") gives the group a name.

Motion

Border and ring transition over --duration-fast; zeroed under reduced motion.

Touch

At least 110px tall and full width: the biggest target on the page.

Content

  • For Nia, label with the question itself and say up front how much to write: "A sentence or two for each one is perfect. Spelling doesn’t matter."
  • For Dad, the help says who reads it: "She reads this on her own, so keep it short and warm."
  • No minimum lengths for Nia; a short answer is still an answer. A counter appears only as an answer nears its 2000-character limit (L2-011).
  • Answers autosave as drafts, so there is no "Save draft" button beside the box.

  <label class="field__label" for="q1"><span class="qa__num">1.</span> What happened in this chapter?</label>
  <textarea class="textarea" id="q1" name="q1" rows="3" required

</div>
