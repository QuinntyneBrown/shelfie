Achievement badge

A small tile for a reading feat: earned with the day it happened, or still to earn with a hint of how. Nia sees them on her shelf; Dad sees the same tiles on Review.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableLibrary: sh-achievement-badge

Overview

Put .achievement in a ul.achievements, one li per badge. There are four badges, always listed in the same order: First book, Double chapter, Weekend reader and Week streak. They are worked out from her chapter logs, so the tile is display only: nothing on it can be pressed.

It appears under "Badges" on her shelf and as "Nia’s badges" on Review.

Anatomy

  1. IconAn emoji in a --control-height-md circle on --color-accent-subtle; decorative, so it is aria-hidden.
  2. Name--text-label. The feat's name, never shortened.
  3. Note--text-body-sm in --color-fg-muted: "Earned Sun 23 Aug", or "Not yet · " and the hint.

States

Two states: earned and locked. A locked badge is also marked by a dashed border and the words "Not yet", so colour is never the only signal.

Responsive behaviour

  • The list is a grid of tiles at least 14rem wide: one column on a phone, two or more from tablet up. Nothing scrolls sideways.
  • Names and notes wrap inside the tile; the icon never shrinks.

Theming

Colours come from semantic tokens only, so both themes work without extra rules. The locked tile uses the sunken surface and a dashed border.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A list of list items. Each item reads as its name and note: "First book, Earned Sun 23 Aug" or "Double chapter, Not yet · Read two chapters in one day". The emoji is decorative.

Keyboard and focus

Not interactive, so not focusable. The section heading ("Badges") gives the list its name.

Content

  • Names are short feats: "First book", "Double chapter", "Weekend reader", "Week streak".
  • Earned notes give the day: "Earned Sun 23 Aug". Locked notes start "Not yet · " and say what to do, in the reader's voice: "Read two chapters in one day".
  • The section badge counts them: "2 of 4".
