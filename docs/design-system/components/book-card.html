Book card

A cover beside the book's title, author, how far Nia has got, and one button for what to do next: "Log Chapter 12". It is the building block of her shelf.

Overview

Use a book card wherever one book is the subject: each book on the shelf, the header of a book page, the header of Log a chapter, and the book being rewarded or removed in a dialog. For many books in a compact column, use a list row with a small cover; for a finished book on the trophy shelf, use a trophy.

  • Shelf card: inside a .card, with progress and one action.
  • Page header: no card, large cover, the title is the page's <h1>.
  • Compact: small cover, title and one line, no action. Used in dialogs and the "finished" card on Review.

Anatomy

  1. CoverA book cover; medium on the shelf, large in a page header, small in compact cards. Decorative.
  2. Title.book-card__title: --text-h4, balanced wrapping. A heading at the right level for its page.
  3. Author and context.book-card__author: --text-body-sm, muted. Author, plus a note such as "Dad added this on Thursday".
  4. Progress row (optional)A progress bar with "11 of 17"; only for books being read.
  5. Actions (optional).book-card__actions: one small button, 8px above.

Sizes

The card's size follows its cover: small (44px) for compact, medium (80px) on the shelf and on Log a chapter, large (120px) in book page headers. The body always fills the rest of the row.

States

The card itself is not a link, so it has no hover or focus; its button does (see button). Its states describe the book.

  • Loading: a skeleton cover beside skeleton lines, inside an aria-busy container.

Responsive behaviour

  • Cover and body stay side by side at every width; the title wraps (balanced) rather than the layout stacking.
  • Shelf cards sit in .grid--auto: one column on phones, two or more once each card can be 18rem wide.
  • Actions wrap below each other if they do not fit; on the shelf there is only one.
  • In a page header the primary "I read Chapter 12" button sits beside the card from 768px and drops below it on phones (.page-header wraps).

Theming

The book card adds no colours of its own: title in --color-fg-default, author in --color-fg-muted, plus whatever the card, cover and progress bar read. It themes through those components.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

On the shelf, an <article> per book with a heading for the title, so screen-reader users can jump book to book. In a page header the title is the <h1>. The cover is aria-hidden; the progress bar is role="progressbar" with aria-valuenow/min/max and an aria-label.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves to the card's button; the card itself is not a stop.
EnterFollows the button's link (log the next chapter).
Screen reader heading keysJump from book title to book title.

Focus

Only the action takes focus, with the shared ring. Do not wrap the whole card in a link: it would make one huge focus target and repeat the title, author and progress in its name.

Labelling

The button names the chapter ("Log Chapter 12"), so it makes sense out of context. The progress bar's label says the same as its visible text ("11 of 17 chapters read").

Motion

The progress bar's width transitions over --duration-slow when it changes; zeroed under prefers-reduced-motion.

Touch

The small button is 32px tall and the only target in the card, so there is nothing to mis-tap next to it.

Content

  • Title exactly as on the book; no truncation on the shelf.
  • Author line: author first, then one short note joined with " · " ("Kate DiCamillo · Dad added this on Thursday").
  • Progress label: "11 of 17" in cards, "11 of 17 chapters" in headers where there is room.
  • The action names the very next chapter: "Log Chapter 12", "Start with Chapter 1".
