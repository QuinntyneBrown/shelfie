Step indicator

Where someone is in a short wizard: a row of dots, one of them stretched, and the words "Step 2 of 5". The text is the information; the dots are decoration.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stablep.steps

Overview

Use a step indicator at the top of every step of a short, linear flow so the person knows how much is left. Shelfie has one such flow: the set-up wizard a parent sees once after signing up, five steps long.

  • Not navigation. The dots are not links or buttons; Back and Next on the step move between steps. For a page that sits inside a hierarchy use a breadcrumb.
  • Not progress. It counts steps, not work. For how far through a book she is, use progress.

Anatomy

  1. Done dots.steps__dot--done: accent at half opacity, one per step already finished.
  2. Current dot.steps__dot--current: full accent and stretched to --space-5 wide, so the eye finds it without reading.
  3. Label"Step 2 of 5" in --text-caption, muted. This is the accessible text; the dots are aria-hidden.

Variants

There is one variant. The number of dots follows the number of steps; Shelfie's wizard has five.

Sizes

Not applicable. The indicator has one size at every width.

States

The indicator is static: no hover, focus, active or disabled, because nothing in it can be pressed. A dot is in one of three states.

Responsive behaviour

  • One row at every width; five dots and the label fit in under 12rem.
  • Centred above the wizard card at every width, with --space-4 below it from the auth card's stack.

Theming

Dots to come use --color-border-strong, the current and done dots the accent, the label the muted foreground: all tokens, so both themes come free. The done dots' half opacity is on the dot, not a second colour.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A paragraph of text. The dots are aria-hidden; the words "Step 2 of 5" carry the meaning and are read as ordinary text at the top of the step. Nothing is focusable, so there is no keyboard map and no focus ring.

Labelling

The step's h1 follows at once, so a screen reader hears "Step 2 of 5. What's your child's name?". The label is never the only place the step is named.

Motion

When the step changes, the current dot stretches over --duration-base. Under reduced motion the tokens zero the duration and it snaps.

Content

  • The label is always "Step N of M", with the step the person is on, never the step just finished.
  • The first and last steps count: the welcome is step 1 and All set is step 5, so the dots fill up as she goes.
