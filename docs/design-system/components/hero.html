Hero

The first thing Nia sees on her shelf: the book she is reading, how far she has got, and one big button that says which chapter she just read.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableNative <section>

Overview

Use the hero once, on the shelf, for "the one thing to do next". It makes logging a chapter one tap from sign-in. When Nia has nothing on the go (shelf, empty) there is no hero; the empty state takes its place. Everything else on a page belongs in a card.

Anatomy

  1. Panelsection.hero: accent tint fading into the surface, hairline border, --radius-lg, --space-6 padding.
  2. CoverA large cover (.cover--lg), decorative.
  3. Eyebrow.hero__eyebrow: "Keep reading" in --text-overline, accent colour.
  4. Title.hero__title on an h2: the book title in --text-h3.
  5. ProgressA large progress row: "11 of 17 chapters".
  6. ActionsA .cluster: the large primary button, then "See all chapters".

Variants

One variant: keep reading. The full shelf version has a ghost link to the book page next to the main button.

Sizes

One size; it fills the container width. The cover is always .cover--lg (--size-cover-lg).

States

The panel itself is not interactive; its buttons have the button states. It has two content states.

Loading

Skeletons in the hero's own shape, so nothing shifts when the book arrives. aria-busy="true" and a label say what is loading.
  • Default: the book with the most recent log, next chapter in the button.
  • Loading: skeleton cover, title, two lines and a button-sized block.
  • No book on the go: no hero; see the shelf empty state.

Responsive behaviour

  • The panel is a wrapping flex row. The body needs at least 14rem, so on a 360px phone the cover sits above the text; from about 480px they sit side by side.
  • The title wraps (it is never truncated); "The Lion, the Witch and the Wardrobe" takes two lines on a phone.
  • Buttons wrap under each other in the .cluster; the primary stays first.

Theming

The background is linear-gradient(135deg, --color-accent-subtle, --color-bg-surface 70%): a hint of accent in the top-left corner, surface everywhere text sits. The eyebrow uses --color-fg-accent. Both are re-defined in the dark theme, so the gradient stays subtle on the dark surface.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A <section aria-labelledby> named by the book title, so it is a region landmark ("The Lion, the Witch and the Wardrobe, region"). The progress bar uses role="progressbar" with aria-valuenow/min/max.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+Tab"I read Chapter 12", then "See all chapters". The panel is not focusable.
EnterFollows the focused link.

Focus

Buttons use the shared ring. The hero has overflow: hidden but its --space-6 padding keeps the 2px ring with offset fully inside.

Labelling

The eyebrow is visible text, read before the title. The progress bar has aria-label="11 of 17 chapters read", matching the visible "11 of 17 chapters". The cover is aria-hidden because the title says the same thing.

Contrast

Eyebrow on tint
Title on tint
Progress label on tint
Progress bar on track
Primary button label
Focus ring on tint

Motion

The progress bar width eases over --duration-slow when it changes; skeletons shimmer on --duration-loop and stop under reduced motion.

Touch

The main button is large (--control-height-lg, 52px), the biggest target on the shelf.

Content

  • Eyebrow: "Keep reading". Always the same two words.
  • Button: in Nia's voice with the chapter number: "I read Chapter 12". It names the next unlogged chapter.
  • Progress: "11 of 17 chapters", never a percentage.
