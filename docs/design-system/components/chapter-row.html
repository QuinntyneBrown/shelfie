Chapter row

One row per chapter on a book's page. Done chapters show a tick and whether Dad has read them, the next chapter is highlighted and opens the log form, and future chapters wait quietly in grey.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable<a> or <div>

Overview

The chapter row is the book page's table of contents and Nia's map of how far she has come. Chapters are numbered, not titled, so each done row shows the first line of her first answer instead. Rows stack inside a .card--flush. For Dad's view of logged chapters with dates and review status, use the table on Manage a book.

  • Done (.chapter--done): logged. A link to her answers.
  • Next (.chapter--next): the one to read now. A link to the log form, highlighted.
  • Future (.chapter--future): not yet reachable. A plain <div>, not a link.

Anatomy

  1. Number bubble.chapter__num: 32px circle. A tick when done, the number otherwise. Green when done, accent when next, grey when future.
  2. Title.chapter__title: "Chapter 9", --text-label.
  3. Meta.chapter__meta: date and the first line of her answer, or "Up next · tap when you’ve read it".
  4. Status.chapter__status: "⭐ Dad read it", "Waiting for Dad" with a clock, or a chevron on the next row.

Sizes

One size: at least 44px tall (--target-comfortable), 12 × 16px padding, a 32px number bubble.

Done and next rows are links and get hover and focus; future rows are inert. Forced here with data-state.

  • Done, reviewed: "⭐ Dad read it". Done, waiting: a clock and "Waiting for Dad"; it links to the pending chapter page.
  • Finished book: every row is done; there is no next row.
  • Loading: rows with a skeleton bubble and one skeleton line, inside an aria-busy container.

Responsive behaviour

  • One flex row at every width. The meta line wraps under the title; the status stays on the right and never wraps (flex: none).
  • At 360px, "Waiting for Dad" plus a long first answer still fits because the meta wraps to two or three lines.
  • On long books, rows after chapter 10 fold into an .accordion--flush ("Chapters 11 to 80") so the page does not become an 80-row scroll.

Theming

The bubble reads --color-success-bg/fg (done), --color-accent and --color-fg-on-accent (next) and --color-bg-subtle with muted ink (future). The next row's background is --color-accent-subtle; future rows dim their text to --color-fg-subtle. All semantic, so both themes work without overrides.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Done and next rows are native links (<a href>); future rows are plain <div>s, so they are read but cannot be activated. The bubble is aria-hidden because the title already says "Chapter 12" and the tick is repeated by the status text.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves through done and next rows in chapter order; future rows are skipped.
EnterOpens the chapter's answers, or the log form for the next chapter.
Enter / Space on the accordion summaryShows or hides the folded chapters.

Focus

The shared ring around the whole row. On the next row it sits on --color-accent-subtle and stays above 3:1.

Labelling

The link's name is its whole text: "Chapter 9, Tue 13 Oct · Edmund got to…, ⭐ Dad read it". The status is words, never just the star or the clock. Do not add a title attribute to the status (the mock does); it is not reliably announced and duplicates the text.

Motion

None on the row. The accordion's plus sign rotates over --duration-base, zeroed under reduced motion.

Touch

The whole row is the target, at least 44px tall and full width. Rows touch, but each is a separate full-width target with a divider between.

Content

  • Title: "Chapter 12". Chapters have no names in Shelfie.
  • Done meta: the day, then the first line of her first answer, in her words: "Tue 13 Oct · Edmund got to the Witch’s house and it was really scary."
  • Next meta: "Up next · tap when you’ve read it".
  • Status: "⭐ Dad read it" or "Waiting for Dad". Written to Nia, not about her.
  • Folded chapters: "Chapters 11 to 80" with "70 more" as a caption.
