Card

A raised surface that holds one group of things: Nia's answers, Dad's feedback form, the chapters waiting for review. Tinted cards mark the happy moments, a finished book and a reward given.

Overview

Use a card to group content that belongs together and has its own title. A card is not a button: Shelfie has no clickable cards; actions inside a card are real buttons and links.

  • Card (.card): 24px padding. Forms, answers, settings sections, stats.
  • Flush (.card--flush): no padding, clips its content. Wraps a list, table or chapter rows edge to edge.
  • Cover tint (automatic): a card whose direct child is a coloured cover takes a wash of that colour (--color-tint-*), so a shelf of book cards reads as a row of books. Nothing to add: .card:has(> .cover--sage) sets --card-bg. Covers nested deeper (in a list row) leave the card white.
  • Success (.card--success): a green tint fading into the surface. A reward was given.
  • Warning (.card--warning): a warm tint. A book is finished and waiting for its reward.
  • Title (.card__title) and stats (.stat) go inside.

Anatomy

  1. Surface--color-bg-surface (or the cover tint), hairline --color-border-default, --radius-lg, --shadow-1, --space-6 padding.
  2. Title.card__title in --text-h4 on a real heading (h2 or h3).
  3. ContentUsually a .stack; here two .stats in a .cluster.

Sizes

One padding (--space-6), or none with .card--flush. Width comes from the layout it sits in.

States

Cards are not interactive, so they have no hover, focus or pressed state. What changes is their content: loading shows skeletons in the same card, and empty shows an empty state inside it.

Responsive behaviour

  • Cards fill their column. On phones every card is full width and stacks; from 1024px they sit in .span-* columns (see layout primitives).
  • Padding stays 24px at every width so content lines up with the page header.
  • Content inside uses .cluster and wraps: the "Give a reward" button drops under the book on a phone.

Theming

The card reads one component token, --card-bg (default --color-bg-surface). The tinted variants draw linear-gradient(135deg, status-bg, --card-bg 70%), so their text always sits on the surface end. In the dark theme the surface darkens and --shadow-1 deepens; the hairline border keeps the edge visible.

/* A card on a sunken area can re-point the token without new CSS */
.card { --card-bg: var(--color-bg-surface); }

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A <section aria-labelledby> when the card is a named region of the page (it becomes a region landmark), an <article> for a self-contained item (a book card), a <form> or <fieldset> when it is a form. No ARIA role on the class itself.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabThe card is not focusable; focus moves through the links and buttons inside it.

Focus

.card--flush clips its content (overflow: hidden); controls inside keep the 2px ring visible because list rows are padded by --space-4.

Labelling

The .card__title is a real heading at the right level and names the section through aria-labelledby. The tint is decorative: the words "finished" and "Rewarded" carry the meaning.

Contrast

Text on card
Muted text and stat labels on card
Text at the tinted corner (success)
Text at the tinted corner (warning)

The card edge (hairline border on canvas) is decorative and not required to meet 3:1.

Motion

None.

Touch

The card itself is not a target. Targets inside follow their own rules (44px buttons, 24px minimum).

Content

  • Titles are short and specific: "Waiting for you", "This week", "Your feedback", "Nia’s answers".
  • Celebration cards lead with the good news: "Matilda is finished!", "Rewarded: Movie night with popcorn".
  • Stat labels are one word ("Chapters", "Streak"); values are numbers, an emoji is allowed.
