Celebration

The moment that makes reading feel like a win: a big sticker, a cheer, how far she has come, and a little confetti. It replaces the form after Nia sends a chapter, and gets bigger when she finishes a book.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableRespects reduced motion

Overview

Use a celebration as the success state of the two big moments in Shelfie, and nowhere else, so it stays special.

  • Chapter logged (Nia): 🎉, "Chapter 12 done!", progress, confetti.
  • Book finished (Nia): the book's cover, 🏆, "You finished the whole book!", more confetti, on a warm .card--warning tint.
  • Feedback sent (Dad): the sticker he gave, "Sent to Nia", and the next chapter to review. No confetti; it is a quiet confirmation.

For small successes (saved settings, a toast after Undo) use a toast or a success alert. The finished-book card on the book page is a card with .card--warning, not a celebration.

Anatomy

  1. Confetti.confetti layer of 8–14 <span>s; each sets --x (left), --d (delay) and --c (a cover colour). Decorative.
  2. Sticker.sticker--xl, 96px: 🎉, 🏆, or the sticker Dad gave.
  3. HeadingThe page <h1>: the cheer.
  4. MessageMuted paragraph, 36ch max: how far, and what happens next.
  5. Progress (chapter only)Large progress row.
  6. ActionsOne large primary ("Back to my shelf") and one secondary.

Examples use <h2>; in the product the heading is the page's <h1>.

Sizes

One size: a full-width card with 48px top and bottom padding. The finished-book variant is taller only because it adds the cover.

  • Playing: each piece falls once over 2.5 s (5 × --duration-deliberate), staggered by its --d. The fall runs once (animation-fill-mode: both), so pieces wait unseen until their delay and rest off-screen afterwards, matching the mock’s “runs 2.5 s then stops”. The app may remove the .confetti layer once the last piece lands.
  • Reduced motion: the confetti layer is hidden entirely; the sticker, heading and message carry the moment.

Responsive behaviour

  • The card is full width in the page container; content is centred and the message stops at 36ch.
  • Confetti positions are percentages, so pieces spread across any width and are clipped to the card.
  • Actions wrap onto two centred rows on phones; the primary stays first.

Theming

The card reads --color-bg-surface; the finished variant adds .card--warning, a gradient from --color-warning-bg into the surface. Confetti colours are the seven --color-cover-* tokens, which have light and dark values, passed in through --c.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A page state, not a dialog. The heading is the page <h1>; the confetti, cover and sticker are aria-hidden. The heading is announced through a live region when the form is replaced, as the log-chapter mock specifies.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabPrimary action, then the secondary.
EnterFollows the link ("Back to my shelf", "I read Chapter 13 too").

Focus

After "I’m done!", focus moves to the celebration heading (tabindex="-1") so keyboard and screen-reader users start at the cheer, with the actions next.

Labelling

The message says the progress in words ("That’s 12 of 17"); the progress bar has its own aria-label. Emoji are hidden, so nothing important is said only by 🎉 or 🏆.

Contrast

Heading on card
Message on card
Primary action
Sticker on rose cover

The warm tint fades into the surface by 70% across the card; the heading and message sit in the lighter part. Confetti is decoration and exempt.

Motion

Confetti is the only big motion in Shelfie. It is short, falls downward, never flashes, and is removed under prefers-reduced-motion. No other part of the card animates.

Touch

The primary action is a 48px button; the secondary is 40px, 12px away. The confetti layer ignores pointer events.

Content

  • Heading: short and excited, about her: "Chapter 12 done!", "You finished the whole book!"
  • Message: how far, then what happens next: "That’s 12 of 17. Only 5 chapters to go — Dad will read your answers soon."
  • Finished book: name the book and promise the reward: "All 21 chapters of Matilda. Dad will pick your reward — watch your shelf."
  • Dad's version is calm and points to the next job: "Sent to Nia", "Review Chapter 10".
