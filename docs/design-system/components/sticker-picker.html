Sticker picker

The row of six stickers Dad picks from when he reviews a chapter. The sticker he picks is the first thing Nia sees on that chapter, so the choice is big, friendly and required.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableNative <button aria-pressed>

Overview

Use the sticker picker only in the review form, where Dad gives one of the six stickers to a chapter Nia logged. Each choice is a .sticker-choice button with an emoji and a word; the picked one has aria-pressed="true". Exactly one can be pressed at a time.

  • The set is fixed: 🌟 Star, 🦉 Wise owl, 🚀 Rocket, 🐉 Dragon, 🍪 Cookie, 💡 Bright idea.
  • The Send feedback dialog uses the same picker for its 1 to 5 score, with five faces in order: 😞 Not fun, 🙁 A bit meh, 😐 Okay, 🙂 Good, 🤩 Love it. The values are "1" to "5", and the score is optional there, so nothing is picked at first. It is a radio group, so the arrow keys move between faces and Tab leaves the group.
  • For the sticker on a book cover use the swatch picker sticker grid. For reward quick picks in the Give a reward dialog use chips. To show a sticker that was already given, use the sticker.

Anatomy

  1. Group.cluster with role="group", named by the "Pick a sticker" label through aria-labelledby.
  2. EmojiFirst child span, --font-size-3xl, aria-hidden="true".
  3. WordSecond span in --text-caption; the accessible name.
  4. Choicebutton.sticker-choice: at least 72px wide and 44px tall, --radius-md, 1px --color-border-strong.

Variants

One variant: the review form's six stickers. The example below is Dad's form for Chapter 11 of The Lion, the Witch and the Wardrobe, with Star picked.

Pick a sticker
Pressing another sticker un-presses Star; pressing Star again leaves it picked.

Sizes

One size. Each choice is at least 4.5rem (72px) wide and --target-comfortable (44px) tall, with --space-2 padding; the emoji is --font-size-3xl so it reads as a sticker, not an icon. Longer words ("Bright idea") widen the button rather than wrap.

States

Hover, focus and active are forced with data-state; real pages get them from :hover, :focus-visible and :active. Pressed, invalid and disabled use the real attributes.

  • Pressed tints the button with --color-accent-subtle, thickens the accent border and colours the word with --color-fg-accent. Shape changes as well as colour.
  • Invalid is set on every choice with aria-invalid="true" and aria-describedby pointing at the error under the row. The form's error summary links to the group label ("Pick a sticker"). Picking any sticker clears it.
  • Disabled is used while the review is sending (review-chapter, submitting): every choice gets disabled and the picked one stays pressed so Dad can see what he sent.
  • Hover lifts the choice by 2px with a spring ease; active drops it back.

Responsive behaviour

  • The row is a .cluster, so it wraps: three a row at 360px, all six on one row in the 5-column feedback card from 1024px.
  • Buttons keep their size at every width; the gap is --space-3.
  • On phones the feedback form sits under Nia's answers; from 1024px it moves to a sticky aside so the stickers stay in view while Dad reads.

Theming

The choice reads semantic tokens only: --color-bg-surface, --color-border-strong and --color-fg-default at rest; --color-accent-subtle, --color-accent and --color-fg-accent when pressed; --color-danger-solid when invalid. The dark theme swaps those tokens and nothing else; the emoji keep their own colours in both themes.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Native <button type="button"> with aria-pressed: the WAI-ARIA APG Button pattern (toggle button), grouped with role="group". Script keeps exactly one pressed. Each button is its own tab stop, which keeps the mock's behaviour and lets Dad reach any sticker directly.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves through the six stickers in order, then on to the note field.
Enter, SpacePicks the focused sticker (aria-pressed="true") and un-presses the others. Pressing the picked one again keeps it picked.

Focus

The shared ring (--color-focus-ring, 2px, 2px offset) on :focus-visible. Focus stays on the button after picking. When the form fails validation, focus goes to the error summary, whose link moves focus to the group label.

Labelling

The word is the accessible name ("Wise owl, toggle button, pressed"); the emoji is aria-hidden. The group is named by the visible "Pick a sticker" label. The error is attached to every choice with aria-describedby, so it is read whichever sticker has focus.

Motion

Background and border fade over --duration-fast; the hover lift uses --ease-spring. Reduced motion zeroes the duration, so the lift and fade become instant.

Touch

Every choice is at least 72×44px with 12px between neighbours. No hover-only information: the word is always visible.

Content

  • The label asks for one thing: "Pick a sticker".
  • Sticker words are one or two plain words in sentence case: "Wise owl", "Bright idea".
  • The error says why it matters to Nia, not what the form needs: "Pick one so Nia knows you read it."
  • Score faces read as a feeling, not a grade: "Not fun" to "Love it", never "1 star".
  • The summary title is gentle when only one thing is missing: "One more thing".
