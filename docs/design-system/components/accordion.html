Accordion

A row that opens to show more. Shelfie uses it for one job: folding away the chapters of a very long book so the page stays short until Nia wants them.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableNative <details>

Overview

Use an accordion to hide content that most people will not need on this visit, and that is long enough to push important things off screen. On the book page, The Wild Robot (80 chapters) shows chapters 1 to 10 and folds 11 to 80 into "Chapters 11 to 80 · 70 more".

Do not use it to hide things Nia needs to act on, like her questions, and do not use it for navigation. The element is always details.accordion.

Anatomy

  1. Containerdetails.accordion: surface, hairline border, --radius-md. Flush variant: top border only.
  2. Summarysummary: label in --text-label plus an optional count in .text-caption; 44px tall.
  3. IndicatorA "+" drawn by CSS in --color-fg-subtle; rotates 45° to "×" when open.
  4. Body.accordion__body, muted text, padded to line up with the summary.

Variants

Flush (in a list)

The product use: inside a .card--flush, below the visible chapter rows, divided by a top border only.

  • Open turns the "+" into "×" by rotating it. The body appears directly below; nothing else moves.
  • Disabled does not exist: if there is nothing to show, do not render the accordion.

Responsive behaviour

  • Full width of its container at every size; the summary label wraps before the indicator moves.
  • On phones it matters most: 70 chapter rows would be several screens of scrolling.

Theming

Reads --color-bg-surface, --color-border-default, --color-bg-subtle (hover), --color-fg-subtle (indicator) and --color-fg-muted (body). All have dark values; the component CSS does not change.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Native <details>/<summary>, which the browser exposes as the WAI-ARIA APG Disclosure pattern (a button with expanded or collapsed state). No ARIA needed. Content inside a closed details is still found by the browser's find-in-page, which opens it.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves to the summary; when open, on into any links inside the body.
Enter, SpaceOpens or closes it.

Focus

The shared ring on the summary. Focus stays on the summary when it opens or closes.

Labelling

The summary text is the name and says what is inside and how much: "Chapters 11 to 80, 70 more". The "+" is CSS content on ::after and is not announced; the expanded state is.

Motion

The indicator rotates over --duration-base. The body does not animate. Reduced motion zeroes the rotation time.

Touch

The whole summary row is the target: full width and 44px tall.

Content

  • Name the range and the count: "Chapters 11 to 80" with "70 more" as a caption.
  • Keep what matters now outside: the done chapters and the next chapter stay visible above the fold-away.
