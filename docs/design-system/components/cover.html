Book cover

Every book gets a made-up cover: a pastel tile in a 2:3 book shape with one big sticker. Nia picks both when a book is added, so The Lion, the Witch and the Wardrobe is always the sage tile with the lion. A book can also have a cover picture, such as a photo of the real cover, which shows over the tile.

Overview

Covers are made, not fetched: there is no online lookup. A grown-up or the reader may add a picture with the cover picker; the colour and sticker stay underneath and show whenever there is no picture, while it loads, or if it fails. The cover gives each book a face a child can find at a glance, and it appears wherever the book does: the shelf, the book page, lists, the review queue, dialogs and the finished-book celebration. People get an avatar, not a cover; rewards get a sticker.

Anatomy

  1. StickerOne emoji, 40% of the tile's width, centred.
  2. Spine shadowA 4px inset on the left edge in 10% --color-fg-on-cover, so the tile reads as a book.
  3. Picture (optional)img.cover__img with alt="", filling the tile with object-fit: cover and clipped to its radius; it fades in over the sticker.
  4. Tile2:3 aspect ratio, width from --size-cover-*, a cover colour, --shadow-1, --radius-sm at small and --radius-md from medium up, like a real book.

Variants

Seven colours, all pastel, all with dark ink. The colour has no meaning beyond "this book"; never use it for status.

Picture

🐕 🦁
A cover picture over the sun and sage tiles. The stored picture is a 2:3, 320 × 480 WebP, so it fills every size without letterboxing.
HTML
<span class="cover cover--sun" aria-hidden="true">🐕<img class="cover__img" src="/api/books/{id}/cover?v={version}" alt=""></span>

On Add a book, the swatch picker sets the same colours through the --cover property (style="--cover: var(--color-cover-mint)"); a saved book always uses the modifier class.

Sizes

Pick the size by how much the book is the subject of the screen.

🦁 🦁 🦁 🦁
Small · 44px (lists, tables, dialogs) · medium · 80px (book cards, log-a-chapter header) · large · 120px (book page, shelf hero) · extra large · 160px (finished-book celebration, Add a book preview).

States

A cover is not interactive: no hover, focus, active or disabled state. Its link or button lives on the card or row around it. The other states are loading and, for a cover with a picture, the picture loading or failing.

Both themes

🦁📚🕷️🐕🐉🤖🚀🐕
🦁📚🕷️🐕🐉🤖🚀🐕
  • Loading: .cover.skeleton keeps the tile's size and shape, drops the shadow and shimmers. Use it with skeleton text beside it, inside an aria-busy container.
  • Picture loading: the colour and sticker show until the picture arrives, then it fades in on --duration-base.
  • Picture failed: an image that fails to load is removed, so the colour and sticker show. There is never a broken-image icon.

Responsive behaviour

  • Sizes are fixed tokens, not fluid. The book page uses the large cover at every width; the title beside it wraps.
  • The cover never shrinks in a flex row (flex: none).
  • In the shelf hero the cover sits beside the text when there is room and above it on narrow phones, because the hero wraps.

Theming

Cover colours (--color-cover-*) and their ink (--color-fg-on-cover) stay the same in both themes, so a book looks the same day and night. The component reads one property, --cover, which each modifier sets; to add an eighth colour, add a --color-cover-* token and its contrast pair, then a .cover--{name} modifier.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A decorative <span aria-hidden="true">. The book's title is always printed next to it, so the cover adds nothing for a screen reader. A cover picture is an <img alt=""> inside that hidden span, so it is decorative too.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabSkips the cover; focus goes to the card's or row's link.

Focus

Never focusable.

Labelling

None: aria-hidden. If a cover ever appears without its title beside it, it needs role="img" and an aria-label with the title; no mock does this.

Emoji draw in their own colours; the ink pair matters when a platform falls back to a monochrome glyph.

Motion

The skeleton shimmer, on --duration-loop, and the picture's fade-in, on --duration-base; both stop under prefers-reduced-motion.

Touch

Not a target on its own. The surrounding card or row is.

Content

  • One emoji per cover, chosen to match the book: 🦁 for the lion, 🤖 for the robot, 🐕 for Winn-Dixie.
  • No text on the cover tile. The title is always next to it. A cover picture may carry the real cover's own lettering.
  • A cover picture is a photo of the book's own cover, never a person.
  • Keep a book's colour and sticker the same everywhere it appears.
