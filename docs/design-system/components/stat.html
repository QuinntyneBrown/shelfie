Stat

A big number with a small label above it. Dad's "This week" card leads with two: chapters Nia read and her streak.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable<div>

Overview

Use a stat when one number answers the question by itself: "How much did she read this week?" Put two or three side by side in a .cluster, never more. A number that needs a comparison or a trend over time is out of scope: Shelfie has no charts. Progress through a book is a progress bar, not a stat.

Anatomy

  1. Label.stat__label: --text-label in muted ink. One or two words.
  2. Value.stat__value: --text-h2 with tabular numerals, so digits do not shift as they change.

Variants

One variant. A value can lead with an emoji when the label alone is not enough for a quick glance.

HTML
<div class="cluster" style="gap: var(--space-6);">
  <div class="stat"><span class="stat__label">Chapters</span><span class="stat__value">5</span></div>
  <div class="stat"><span class="stat__label">Streak</span><span class="stat__value">🔥 5</span></div>
</div>

Sizes

One size: the value is --text-h2, the label --text-label, 4px apart.

States

Stats are read-only text: no hover, focus, active or disabled state.

  • Zero shows "0", not a dash: a quiet week is still an answer.
  • Loading: the whole "This week" card is skeleton lines until the data arrives (review/loading).

Responsive behaviour

  • Stats sit in a wrapping .cluster; two always fit on one line, even at 360px.
  • The card holding them is the right-hand column (.span-4) from 1024px and full width below.

Theming

Reads only --color-fg-muted (label) and inherits --color-fg-default (value) from the card. Nothing to override per theme.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Two spans in a <div>, read in order: "Chapters 5". No role. The label comes first in the DOM so the number is never read without its meaning.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabSkips stats; there is nothing to operate.

Focus

Never focusable.

Labelling

The visible label names the number. The card heading ("This week") sets the time frame, so the label does not repeat it. An emoji in the value (🔥) is read aloud as "fire"; that is acceptable here because the label already says "Streak".

Motion

None. Numbers do not count up.

Touch

Not a target.

Content

  • Label: one or two words, sentence case, a noun ("Chapters", "Streak").
  • Value: a numeral. Units only when the label does not imply them.
  • The time frame lives in the heading above, once.
