Progress

A rounded bar that fills as Nia reads through a book: 11 of 17 chapters is two thirds full. It turns green when the book is finished.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stablerole="progressbar"

Overview

Use the progress bar for one thing: how many chapters of a book are logged. It always has a known value, so there is no indeterminate bar; for waiting, use a skeleton or a loading button.

  • Pair it with a label in a .progress-row ("11 of 17 chapters") so the number is visible, not only the length.
  • It appears in the shelf hero, on book cards, on the book page, in the log-chapter celebration and in Dad's book list.

Anatomy

  1. Fill.progress__bar; width from --value (a percentage), accent colour.
  2. Track.progress; 8px (12px with --lg), fully rounded, --color-bg-subtle-hover.
  3. Label.progress-row__label: "11 of 17", tabular figures, never wraps.

Variants

Two variants: in progress (accent) and complete (success green).

11 of 17 chapters
21 of 21 · finished!
The Lion, the Witch and the Wardrobe in progress; Matilda finished.
HTML
<div class="progress-row">
  <div class="progress progress--lg progress--complete" role="progressbar"
       aria-valuenow="21" aria-valuemin="0" aria-valuemax="21" aria-label="21 of 21 chapters read">
    <div class="progress__bar" style="--value: 100%;"></div>
  </div>
  <span class="progress-row__label">21 of 21 · finished!</span>
</div>

Sizes

Default (8px) sits in book cards and list rows. Large (12px) is for the one book a view is about: the shelf hero, the book page and the celebration.

  • Not started shows the empty track and "0 of 26" (Because of Winn-Dixie), so the book still looks like something to begin.
  • Value changes (after logging a chapter) animate the fill over --duration-slow.
  • Complete adds .progress--complete at 100%; the label adds "· finished!".

Responsive behaviour

  • The track fills whatever width is left in its row; the label keeps its width and never wraps.
  • On phones, book cards use the short label ("11 of 17"); the hero and book page can afford "11 of 17 chapters".
  • At 4% (3 of 80) the fill is only a few pixels; the label makes the value clear at any width.

Theming

The track reads --color-bg-subtle-hover, the fill --color-accent, the complete fill --color-success-solid, and the label --color-fg-muted. The value is a custom property, --value, set on the bar, so the component needs no inline width.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

The track is role="progressbar" with aria-valuenow, aria-valuemin="0", aria-valuemax (the chapter count) and an aria-label in words ("11 of 17 chapters read"). WAI-ARIA progressbar (meter-like, read only).

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabNot a stop; the bar is read only.
Arrow keys (screen reader)Reads "11 of 17 chapters read, progress bar".

Focus

None.

Labelling

Use chapter numbers, not percentages, in aria-label, matching the visible label. The visible label repeats the value; it is fine that a screen reader hears it twice.

Contrast

Fill on track
Complete fill on track
Label on card

Motion

The fill width transitions over --duration-slow; reduced motion zeroes it, so the new value appears at once.

Touch

No targets.

Content

  • Label: "{logged} of {total}", optionally "chapters": "11 of 17 chapters".
  • Finished: "21 of 21 · finished!"
  • aria-label: "{logged} of {total} chapters read".
