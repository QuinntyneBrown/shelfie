Reading goal card

A household’s or a community’s reading goal: how many books, by when, and how far along.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: newLibrary: sh-goal-card
  • Overview
  • Anatomy
  • States
  • Responsive behaviour
  • Accessibility
  • Content
  • Tokens
  • Code
  • Sources

Overview

A card for a Reading goal: a number of finished books by an end date, for the household or a community (ADR-0021). It is built on Progress and shown on the shelf (read only), on Community and on a community (with Edit and Remove for the parents who may change it).

It is never used for the reward gallery’s goal, which keeps the Trophy and the words “Reading toward”.

Anatomy

  1. Icon: 🎯 household, 🤝 community, 🏆 reached, ⏳ ended; decorative.
  2. Title, with a Reached or Ended badge.
  3. Note: the target, the end date and who counts.
  4. Progress: sh-progress, “4 of 10”.
  5. Actions (optional, parents only): Edit and Remove.

Responsive behaviour

  • The body takes at least 12rem; on a phone the actions wrap under it, right-aligned.
  • The note wraps; the icon never shrinks.

Accessibility

The title is a heading (h3 under the section’s h2). The bar is a progressbar named “4 of 10 books finished”. Reached and ended are said in words by the badge. Edit and Remove are buttons named for the goal (“Edit our reading goal”).

Content

  • Always “Reading goal”: “Our reading goal”, “Set a reading goal”, “Reading goal reached!”.
  • Never “Your goal” or “Reading toward”; those belong to the reward gallery.
  • Notes: “10 books by Thu 31 Dec · Nia and Theo together”. A community card never names another family’s readers or books.

Tokens

--color-bg-surface, --color-border-default, --color-accent-subtle; reached --color-success-bg / --color-success-border; ended --color-bg-surface-sunken; --radius-lg, --space-3, --space-4.

Code

Angular: <sh-goal-card [title] [note] [finished] [target] [state]> with projected action buttons. Classes: .goal-card, .goal-card--reached, .goal-card--ended, __icon, __body, __title, __note, __progress, __actions.
