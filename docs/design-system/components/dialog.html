Dialog

A focused task on top of the page: give Nia her reward, confirm removing a book, add a question. It holds focus until it is done or cancelled, and becomes a bottom sheet on phones.

Overview

Use a dialog for a short task that belongs to the page underneath and must be finished or cancelled before going on. Shelfie has three, all for Dad:

  • Give a reward: a form dialog with a field, quick-pick chips and an optional note. Opens from Review and Manage a book when a book is finished.
  • Remove a book: a destructive confirmation with a danger icon and a danger button.
  • Add or edit a question: a form dialog with a textarea and a radio group.

Do not use a dialog for messages (use a toast or alert), for navigation, or for anything Nia does. The account menu is a menu, not a dialog.

Anatomy

  1. Backdrop and frame.backdrop dims the page with --color-bg-backdrop at --z-overlay; .dialog (role="dialog", aria-modal="true") centres the panel at --z-modal.
  2. Icon (destructive only).dialog__icon--danger: 40px circle, danger tint.
  3. Titleh2.dialog__title, the dialog's name via aria-labelledby. --text-h4 on phones, --text-h3 from 640px.
  4. CloseGhost icon button, aria-label="Close".
  5. Body.dialog__body, usually a .stack; scrolls inside the panel when tall.
  6. Footer.dialog__footer: sticky, right-aligned, safe action first, primary or danger last.

Variants

In-page examples use .dialog--static, a documentation hook that renders the dialog in flow. They carry role="dialog" but not aria-modal, so this page stays readable; the product sets both.

Form: add a question

Add a question

Nia answers it for every chapter of The Lion, the Witch and the Wardrobe. Chapters she has already logged keep their old questions.
Where it goes
Editing opens the same dialog titled "Edit question" with the text filled in.

Sizes

One panel width, up to 32rem; height grows with content up to the viewport minus --space-12, then the body scrolls and the footer stays.

States

Four states, the same across all three dialogs. The controls inside use their own real attributes.

Busy

Busy

Invalid

Finish the question — at least a few words, ending in a question mark helps.

Failed

The book wasn’t removed
Shelfie couldn’t reach the server. Nothing has changed; try again.
  • Default: focus on the first field (form dialogs) or the safe button (destructive).
  • Busy: fields readonly, chips and radios disabled, both footer buttons disabled, the pressed one also aria-busy="true". Escape and the close button do nothing until it settles.
  • Invalid: the field gets aria-invalid="true" and a .field__error linked with aria-describedby; focus moves to it. The dialog stays open. Give a reward: "Tell Nia what the reward is."
  • Failed: a danger alert with role="alert" at the top of the body; what Dad typed stays. Copy: "The reward wasn’t saved", "The question wasn’t saved", "The book wasn’t removed".

Responsive behaviour

  • Below 640px (--layout-breakpoint-sm) the panel is a bottom sheet: full width, pinned to the bottom, top corners --radius-xl, sliding up over --duration-slow.
  • From 640px it is centred with --space-6 around it, all corners --radius-lg, scaling in; the title grows to --text-h3.
  • The footer is sticky, so the actions stay in reach when the body scrolls or the phone keyboard is up. Buttons wrap, primary last.

Add a question

Bottom sheet at 360px: rounded top corners only, full width. Real pages get this from the base styles, no modifier needed.

Theming

The panel is --color-bg-surface-raised with --shadow-4, over --color-bg-backdrop. In the dark theme the raised surface is lighter than the page, so the panel still lifts off a dark backdrop; the backdrop itself gets darker. The danger icon reads --color-danger-bg and --color-danger-icon.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

The WAI-ARIA APG Dialog (Modal) pattern: role="dialog", aria-modal="true", aria-labelledby pointing at the title and, when there is a description, aria-describedby. The page behind is inert while it is open.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves through the controls inside and wraps from the last to the first; focus never leaves the dialog.
EscCancels and closes (same as "Not now", "Cancel" or "Keep the book"). Ignored while busy.
Enter, SpaceActivates the focused button or chip.
↑ ↓Moves between the "Where it goes" radios.

Focus

On open, focus goes to the first field (The reward, Question) or, for Remove a book, to "Keep the book" so a stray Enter is safe. On close, focus returns to the button that opened it (Give a reward, Remove, Add a question). On invalid, focus moves to the first invalid field; on failed, the alert is announced and focus stays where it was.

Labelling

The title names the dialog and says the subject: "Matilda is finished! 🏆", "Remove “The Lion, the Witch and the Wardrobe”?". The close button is aria-label="Close". The danger icon is aria-hidden; the title and the danger button's words carry the warning.

Motion

The backdrop fades in over --duration-base; the sheet slides up (phones) or the panel scales in from 96% (wider) over --duration-slow with --ease-enter. Reduced motion zeroes both, so it simply appears.

Touch

Footer buttons are 44px tall with 12px between them; the close button is 44px square. The sheet keeps the actions at the bottom, in thumb reach.

Content

  • Titles say the subject; confirmation titles are a question naming the thing: "Remove “The Lion, the Witch and the Wardrobe”?"
  • Buttons are specific verbs and the safe one is a real choice: "Keep the book" / "Remove book and answers", "Not now" / "Give the reward", "Cancel" / "Add question". Never "OK" or "Yes".
  • Destructive bodies name what is lost in bold and say "This can’t be undone." Offer the better path when there is one ("give a reward instead").
  • Failure messages say what did not happen and that nothing was lost: "Shelfie couldn’t reach the server. What you typed is still here."
