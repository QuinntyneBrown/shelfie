Chip

A pill-shaped button for a quick, low-stakes choice. Nia taps "Today" or "Yesterday" when she logs a chapter; Dad taps "Movie night" to fill in a reward.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableNative <button>

Overview

Use chips for a short row of choices that are faster to tap than to type. Use a radio group when each choice needs a sentence of explanation, and a button when the action submits or changes something on its own. A chip never saves anything by itself.

  • Toggle chip (aria-pressed): one of a small set is on. "When did you read it?" with Today, Yesterday and Another day.
  • Suggestion chip (no aria-pressed): tapping fills a nearby field. The reward quick picks.

A chip that labels a status and cannot be tapped is a badge.

Anatomy

  1. Label--text-label, one to four words. The accessible name.
  2. Pill32px tall (--control-height-sm), 12px side padding, --radius-full, 1px --color-border-strong. Accent tint and border when pressed.

Variants

Toggle chips

From Log a chapter. Pressing one chip un-presses the others. The last chip holds a date field for older days.
HTML
<div class="cluster" role="group" aria-label="Day you read it">
  <button class="chip" type="button" aria-pressed="true">Today</button>
  <button class="chip" type="button" aria-pressed="false">Yesterday</button>
  <label class="chip">Another day
    <input class="input input--sm" type="date" value="2026-10-17" aria-label="Pick the day"></label>
</div>

Suggestion chips

From Give a reward. Tapping a pick writes it into "The reward" field, where Dad can still edit it.
HTML
<div class="cluster" role="group" aria-label="Quick picks">
  <button class="chip" type="button">Ice cream Saturday</button>
  <button class="chip" type="button">Movie night</button>
</div>

Sizes

One size, 32px tall. Chips sit in a .cluster with 12px between them, which keeps neighbouring targets apart.

States

Pointer and focus states are forced with data-state; real pages get them from :hover, :focus-visible, aria-pressed and disabled.

Toggle
SuggestionNot usedNot used

Both themes

  • Pressed uses aria-pressed="true": accent tint, accent border, accent label.
  • Disabled uses the native attribute at 50% opacity. Chips are disabled only while their form is sending (the submit button shows aria-busy), so a tap cannot change the answer mid-send.
  • There is no loading or invalid chip; errors belong to the field the chip fills.

Responsive behaviour

  • Chips wrap onto new lines inside the .cluster; they never scroll sideways and never shrink.
  • Labels do not wrap inside a chip. At 360px the four reward picks take two lines, which is fine.
  • The "Another day" chip keeps its date field inline at every width; the browser's own date picker opens on phones.

Theming

Unpressed chips read --color-bg-surface, --color-border-strong and --color-fg-default; hover adds --color-bg-subtle. Pressed reads --color-accent-subtle, --color-accent and --color-fg-accent. All are semantic tokens, so both themes come for free.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Native <button type="button">. Toggle chips follow the APG button pattern's toggle form (aria-pressed); the set sits in role="group" with an aria-label. The group behaves like a single choice, so the script that presses one chip un-presses the others.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves chip to chip; every chip is a tab stop.
Enter / SpacePresses a toggle chip, or fills the field from a suggestion chip.

Focus

The shared 2px focus ring with 2px offset, which stays at least 3:1 on the pressed accent tint. After a suggestion chip fills the field, focus stays on the chip.

Labelling

The visible text is the name. Screen readers announce "Today, toggle button, pressed". The group's aria-label ("Day you read it", "Quick picks") gives the context, and the date field inside "Another day" has its own aria-label, "Pick the day".

Motion

Background colour fades over --duration-fast (120 ms), zeroed under prefers-reduced-motion.

Touch

32px tall with 12px between chips: above the 24px minimum, and the gap keeps a thumb from pressing two. Nia's day chips are the first thing on the log form, so they are easy to reach.

Content

  • One to four words, sentence case: "Today", "Another day", "Stay up 30 min later".
  • Suggestions are the reward itself, exactly as it should appear on Nia's shelf.
  • Order by likelihood: "Today" first, then "Yesterday", then "Another day".
  • No more than four or five chips in a group; more than that is a field.
