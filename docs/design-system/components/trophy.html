Component · Shelfie

Trophy

A finished book on Nia's trophy shelf: the small cover, the title, the reward Dad gave, and a sticker for the reward. It is the payoff at the bottom of her shelf. The same row shows a reward from Dad's gallery that she can pick and read toward. In Angular it is sh-reward-card.

Overview

Use a trophy only in the "Finished" section of the shelf, one per finished book, newest first. A book she is still reading is a book card; a book in Dad's lists is a list row.

  • The reward line is the point. It shows the reward Dad typed ("Movie night with popcorn") or, until he gives one, that it is coming.
  • The trophy is not a link in the mocks. Nia reaches a finished book from its cover elsewhere; the trophy shelf is a list to admire.

Anatomy

  1. CoverCover at .cover--sm, aria-hidden; the title says which book.
  2. Title.trophy__title: label font, one line, ellipsis; full title in title.
  3. Reward.trophy__reward: the reward, or "Finished Tue 13 Oct · Dad is picking your reward".
  4. Sticker40px sticker for the reward; 🏆 while it is coming. Decorative.
  5. Container<li>, surface, hairline border, --radius-lg, 12px padding.

Gallery reward

The same row, with .trophy--gallery, shows a reward from Dad's gallery under "Reading toward" on her shelf (sh-reward-card with a target). Its featured picture takes the cover's place in a large sticker (.sticker--lg with .sticker__img); the 🎁 shows underneath while it loads, and stays when the reward has no picture or the picture fails to load (the image is removed, never shown broken). The second line says what it takes, Dad's description follows in .trophy__description when he wrote one (and before the progress bar on her goal), and a small .btn--sm "Pick this" sits in .trophy__actions. The reward she picked gets .trophy--goal (accent border), a "Your goal" badge and a progress bar of the books she has finished since she picked it. At the target the badge reads "Ready!", and the page shows a celebration above the list and hides the pick buttons.

  • Responsive: the body takes at least 12rem, so under about 360px the pick button wraps under the text, right-aligned.
  • Accessibility: the button's name includes the reward ("Pick Trip to the zoo") because every row says "Pick this". The progress bar is a role="progressbar" named "1 of 3 books finished"; the badge and note repeat it in text, so the state never rests on the border colour.
  • Content: the name is exactly what Dad typed (1 to 100 characters), the description too (0 to 500, line breaks kept); the note reads "Finish 3 books", then "1 of 3 books finished · 2 more to go!", then "3 of 3 books finished · you did it!".
  • The picture is decorative (alt="" inside the aria-hidden sticker): the name is printed beside it. Readers only see pictures; Dad adds them in Settings with the picture list.
  • Tokens: the goal border is --color-accent, doubled with a hairline box-shadow; the bar is the progress bar's --color-accent. No new tokens.

Sizes

One size: a small cover (40px wide) and a 40px sticker in a row as wide as the column.

States

The trophy is not interactive, so it has no hover, focus, active or disabled state.

  • Narrow column: a title longer than the space truncates to one line with an ellipsis. The full title stays in the DOM, so screen readers read all of it; the title attribute is a pointer-only extra.
  • The reward line wraps instead of truncating, so a long reward is never cut off.

Responsive behaviour

  • Trophies stack in one column at every width, 8px apart. The body flexes; cover and sticker keep their size.
  • At 360px the title has about 200px; longer titles truncate, rewards wrap to a second line.
  • The list is never paginated: Nia sees every finished book, newest first.

Theming

The row reads --color-bg-surface, --color-border-default, --color-fg-default for the title and --color-fg-muted for the reward. Cover colours come from --color-cover-*, which are tuned per theme so the sticker on them keeps 4.5:1.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A native list (<ul> of <li class="trophy">) under the "Finished" heading, so a screen reader announces "list, 2 items" before reading each book.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabNot a stop; trophies have no actions.
List navigation (screen reader)Moves item by item: title, then reward.

Focus

None.

Labelling

Cover and sticker are aria-hidden. The title and reward line are text, so the reward is never only an emoji. "Dad is picking your reward" says plainly that the reward is coming.

Contrast

Title on surface

Motion

None.

Touch

No targets. If the trophy ever becomes a link, the whole row is the target (it is already 64px tall).

Content

  • Title: the book's title as added, never shortened by hand.
  • Reward given: exactly what Dad typed, such as "Movie night with popcorn" or "Ice cream Saturday".
  • Reward coming: "Finished Tue 13 Oct · Dad is picking your reward". Say when she finished and that something is on its way.

ClassEffect
.trophyRow: cover, body, sticker; bordered surface.
.trophy__bodyFlexes and shrinks so the title can truncate.
.trophy__title + .truncateOne-line title with ellipsis.
.trophy__rewardMuted small text; wraps.
.trophy--galleryA gallery reward: wraps, body at least 12rem.
.trophy--goalThe reward she is reading toward: accent border.
.trophy__progressSpace above the progress bar inside the body.
.trophy__actionsThe pick button, pushed to the end of the row.
.trophy__descriptionA gallery reward's description: body text under the target, line breaks kept, long words wrap.
.sticker__imgThe featured picture over the 🎁, cropped to the circle.
