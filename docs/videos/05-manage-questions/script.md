# 05 · Manage questions

## Introduction

This is the fifth Shelfie journey: managing questions. Every chapter Nia logs comes with comprehension questions. Three are built in, and Dad can add his own to any book.

## Writing a question

Dad signs in, opens Books, and presses Manage next to The Lion, the Witch and the Wardrobe.

The questions section lists what Nia answers for every chapter. The first three are default questions: what happened, who was your favourite character and why, and what do you think will happen next. They cannot be edited or removed.

Dad presses Add a question and types: Where did the children go in this chapter? Under the box, a note reminds him that chapters Nia has already logged keep their old questions.

He presses Add question, and it appears as question four, marked Added by you, with its own Edit and Remove buttons. From her next chapter on, Nia answers all four.

## Recap

Things to remember. The three default questions are always asked. Dad’s questions are asked for every new chapter of that book. And editing or removing a question never changes answers Nia has already given.
