# 14 · Manage a book

## Introduction

This video shows how Dad looks after the books on Nia's shelf: fixing a book's details, or removing one.

## Editing a book

Dad signs in and opens Books from the top bar. Every book shows its progress, status and a Manage link. Because of Winn-Dixie went in with twelve chapters, but the real book has twenty-six. Dad opens Manage, types the new count and presses Save details. Saved just now, and Nia sees the change on her shelf.

Next he manages The Lion, the Witch and the Wardrobe. Its logged chapters are listed newest first, marked To review or Reviewed. If Dad tries to set the chapters below eleven, an inline error explains that Nia has already logged eleven chapters, and nothing is saved.

## Removing a book

To remove a book, Dad presses Remove book at the bottom of the page. A dialog asks if he is sure, with Keep the book already in focus. He presses Remove book and answers, lands back on Books, and a toast confirms the book was removed from the shelf.

## Recap

Things to remember. The chapter count never goes below the chapters already logged. Removing a book also deletes its chapters and answers, and it cannot be undone.
