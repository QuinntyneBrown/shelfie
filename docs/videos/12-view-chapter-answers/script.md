# 12 · View chapter answers

## Introduction

Once Nia logs a chapter, her answers are not gone. She can open any logged chapter to read them again.

## Reading back her answers

From the book page, Nia taps chapter ten. The page says Chapter ten, and that she read it on Thursday the fifteenth of October. Dad has not read this one yet, so a card says his sticker and note will show up here when he does.

Under Your answers, each question sits above what she wrote. Nothing here can be changed; it is just for reading. A button takes her on to chapter eleven, and Back to the book returns her to the list.

## Seeing Dad's feedback

When Dad reviews a chapter, Nia hears about it on her shelf. A banner says Dad read Chapter eleven and gave you a Wise owl. She taps Open Chapter eleven, and there is Dad's sticker with his note: Brilliant, Nia! Once she has opened it, the banner goes away.

## Recap

Things to remember. Her answers are for reading, not editing. And Dad's sticker and note appear on the chapter, with a banner on the shelf until she opens it.
