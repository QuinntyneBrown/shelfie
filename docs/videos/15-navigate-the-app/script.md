# 15 · Navigate the app

## Introduction

This video shows how to get around Shelfie, on a big screen and on a phone. The screen changes size, but the way around stays the same.

## On a desktop

When Dad signs in, he lands on Review. The top bar holds the Shelfie brand, then his three places: Review, Books and Settings. A small badge on Review counts the chapters waiting for him. He clicks Books, then Settings, then Review again, and the page he is on is always marked. On the right, his account menu shows his e-mail, with Settings, Switch reader and Sign out. Escape closes it again.

## On a phone

On a phone, the same three places move to a tab bar along the bottom edge, right under his thumb. He taps Books, then Settings, then Review. The top links step aside, nothing scrolls sideways, and every control is at least forty-four pixels square, so it is easy to tap.

## Recap

Things to remember. Grown-ups get Review, Books and Settings, at the top on wide screens and at the bottom on phones. Nia's screens stay simpler, with just the brand and her account menu.
