# 10 · Sign in with password

## Introduction

This video shows how Dad signs in. Nia uses a four-digit PIN, but a grown-up signs in with an e-mail address and a password.

## Signing in with a password

The first time on a new browser, Shelfie says Welcome back and asks for an e-mail and a password. Dad types dad at example dot com, then his password. Here he slips and types the wrong one. Shelfie just says E-mail or password is wrong, and keeps what he typed so he can fix it.

With the right password, he presses Sign in and lands straight on Review, where he can see how many chapters are waiting for him to read.

The next time he comes back on the same browser, Shelfie remembers him. He picks Dad from the list of names, sees that he is signing in as dad at example dot com, and types only his password.

## Recap

Things to remember. A wrong try gets one simple message that never gives away which part was wrong. On a browser he has used before, Dad only needs his password. And if he ever forgets it, the Forgot your password link sits right under the Sign in button.
