# 13 · Track streak

## Introduction

Nia's reading streak counts how many days in a row she has read. It is a small, friendly nudge to pick up her book every day.

## Watching the streak

When Nia signs in, her streak sits in the top bar next to a little flame. Here she has read on five days in a row, ending today, so it says five days. Her shelf says the same thing right under her greeting: five-day streak. Dad sees it too, on his Review page, under This week.

## How the days count

If she has not read yet today, the streak does not break. With four days in a row up to yesterday, it still shows four, so she has the whole day to keep it going.

But if she misses both today and yesterday, the streak starts again. Here her last reading was two days ago, so the top bar shows zero days, ready for a fresh start.

## Recap

Things to remember. The top bar and the shelf always show the same number. Today does not count against her until it is over. And two missed days in a row send the streak back to zero.
