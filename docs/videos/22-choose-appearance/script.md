# 22 · Choose appearance

## Introduction

This journey is about how Shelfie looks: light, dark, or matching the device. Dad picks it once in Settings, and it applies everywhere.

## Choosing the appearance

Dad signs in with his password on a laptop set to light. He opens Settings and scrolls down to Appearance, which applies on every device for both of them.

He chooses Always dark and presses Save settings. The whole app turns dark at once. When he reloads the page, it stays dark, because the choice is saved for the household, not just for this browser.

Then he goes back to Match the device and saves again. Now Shelfie follows the laptop. When the laptop switches to dark mode, the app turns dark, and when it switches back to light, so does Shelfie.

## Recap

Things to remember. There are three choices: Match the device, Always light and Always dark. Every household starts on Match the device. And because the choice belongs to the household, when Dad picks Always dark, Nia sees dark too when she signs in on another device.
