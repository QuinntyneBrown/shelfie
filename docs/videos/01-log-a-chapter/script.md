# 01 · Log a chapter

## Introduction

This is the first Shelfie journey: logging a chapter. It is what Nia does every time she finishes reading, and it should take her less than a minute.

## Logging the chapter

Nia signs in with her PIN and lands on her shelf. She finds the book she is reading, and presses the button to log the next chapter. Here that is chapter twelve of The Lion, the Witch and the Wardrobe.

First, the form asks when she read it. Today is already chosen, so she can skip straight to the questions.

Question one asks what happened in this chapter. It is the only question she has to answer. A sentence or two is perfect, and spelling does not matter. Questions two and three are optional, but Nia likes to answer them too.

When she presses I'm done, Shelfie saves the chapter and throws a little party. Her progress bar moves to twelve of seventeen, and a message offers Undo for ten seconds, just in case she pressed too soon.

## Recap

Things to remember. Only the first answer is required. Today is the default day, with Yesterday and Another day one tap away. And nothing is final until the Undo message disappears.
