# 17 · Work offline

## Introduction

This video shows what happens when the internet drops out in the middle of reading time. Nia can keep logging, and Shelfie sends everything later.

## Logging without a connection

Nia opens The Lion, the Witch and the Wardrobe and presses I read Chapter twelve. While she types her answer, the Wi-Fi drops. A banner appears at the top: No internet connection. Showing what we saved earlier.

She presses I'm done anyway. A toast says she is offline, and that Chapter twelve is saved on this device and will be sent when she is back online. Back on the book, chapter twelve says Will send.

When the connection returns, the banner goes away by itself. Shelfie sends the kept chapter, and two toasts confirm it: Chapter twelve saved, and Everything you logged has been sent.

## Recap

Things to remember. Pages Nia has already opened can still be read offline. Chapters she logs are kept on her device and sent in the order she logged them. If sending fails, a toast offers Retry.
