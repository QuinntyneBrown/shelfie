# 18 · Stay signed in

## Introduction

This video shows how Shelfie keeps Nia signed in from one day to the next, and how it signs her out safely when it should.

## Staying signed in

Nia signs in with her PIN and lands on her shelf, where it says Hi Nia. If the page is reloaded, nothing changes. She is still signed in, on the same page.

Every time she uses the app, her session runs for another thirty days. Only after thirty quiet days does Shelfie sign her out. When that happens, she sees a note that says You were signed out, and she picks her name to carry on.

## Signing out

When Nia is finished for the day, she opens the account menu and chooses Sign out. The server ends the session straight away, and Shelfie goes back to the picker that asks who's reading today. Opening her shelf now only leads to the sign-in page.

## Recap

Things to remember. Thirty quiet days before Shelfie signs her out, and the clock starts again every time she uses it. Reloading never loses her place. And Sign out ends the session on the server, not just on the screen.
