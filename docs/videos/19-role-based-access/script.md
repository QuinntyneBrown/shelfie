# 19 · Role-based access

## Introduction

Shelfie has two kinds of people: Nia, the reader, and Dad, the grown-up. This video shows how each of them sees only the screens meant for them.

## Nia stays on her shelf

Nia signs in with her PIN and lands on her shelf. The Review pages are where Dad reads her answers, so if she types the Review address, Shelfie shows a friendly lock that says This part is for grown-ups. One tap on Back to my shelf takes her home.

If she follows an old link, she sees We can't find that page, with the same way back to her shelf.

## Dad finds his way back

Dad signs in with his e-mail and password and lands on Review. When he opens a page that does not exist, the same Not found page offers Go to Review instead, because that is his home.

Behind the scenes, every request is checked against the household. Another family's books and answers never appear, even if someone guesses a link to them.

## Recap

Things to remember. Nia never sees the grown-up pages; she gets a friendly lock and a way home. Every error page leads back to the right place for whoever is signed in. And each family only ever sees its own books.
