# 03 · Browse the shelf

## Introduction

This is the third Shelfie journey: browsing the shelf. The shelf is Nia’s home screen. One look should tell her what she is reading, what comes next, and what she has already finished.

## Browsing the shelf

Nia signs in and the shelf says Hi Nia. Her books sit in three groups, and every card shows its cover sticker, its title and how far she has read.

On the go holds the books she is reading right now. The Lion, the Witch and the Wardrobe shows eleven of seventeen chapters read, with a button to log chapter twelve.

Up next holds the books she has not started. Because of Winn-Dixie waits there, with a button to start with chapter one.

Finished is her trophy shelf. Charlotte’s Web earned movie night with popcorn, and Matilda says Dad is picking your reward.

To pick a book, Nia taps its title. The book page shows her progress and one big button: I read chapter twelve.

## Recap

Things to remember. On the go, Up next and Finished answer the three questions Nia asks most. Every card has one clear next step. And when the shelf is empty, it tells her to ask a grown-up to add a book.
