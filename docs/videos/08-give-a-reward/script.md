# 08 · Give a reward

## Introduction

This is journey eight: giving a reward. When Nia finishes a book, Dad picks a reward, and it lands on her shelf next to the book she worked so hard on.

## Giving the reward

Dad signs in, and Review shows that Matilda is finished. Its card has one button, Give a reward, so he presses it.

A dialog opens and says to pick a reward, and Nia will see it on her shelf. The reward box is ready for typing, but there are four quick picks underneath, like Ice cream Saturday and Trip to the bookshop. Dad chooses Movie night, and it fills the box for him.

He presses Give the reward. A message says Reward given, and the Matilda card is gone from Review.

Then Dad switches reader, and Nia signs in with her PIN. Down under Finished, Matilda now shows Movie night. She opens the book, and a trophy card reminds her that she finished Matilda, with her reward right underneath.

## Recap

Things to remember. Each finished book gets one reward. The reward has to say something, and it has to be one hundred characters or fewer. And if saving fails, the dialog keeps what Dad typed so he can try again.
