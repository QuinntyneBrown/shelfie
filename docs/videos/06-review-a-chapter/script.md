# 06 · Review a chapter

## Introduction

This is journey six: reviewing a chapter. Every time Nia logs a chapter, Dad reads her answers and sends back a sticker and a short note. It is how she knows somebody read what she wrote.

## Reviewing the chapter

Dad signs in with his e-mail and password, and Shelfie opens on Review. Chapter eleven of The Lion, the Witch and the Wardrobe is at the top of the list, so he presses Review.

The page shows Nia's answers first. Here she wrote that Father Christmas gave them presents.

Beside her answers is his feedback. He picks a sticker first. There are six to choose from, and today it is the Wise owl. Then he adds a note. The note is optional, and Nia reads it on her own, so short and warm works best.

He presses Send to Nia. Shelfie confirms that she will see his Wise owl and note the next time she opens Shelfie. One more chapter is waiting, so the button to review chapter ten is right there.

## Recap

Things to remember. A sticker is required, the note is not. Notes stop at three hundred characters. And once a chapter is sent, its feedback is read-only, so it cannot be sent twice.
