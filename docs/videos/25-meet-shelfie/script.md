# 25 · Meet Shelfie

## Introduction

Shelfie is a reading app for families. Kids log every chapter they read, and the grown-ups who love them cheer each one on.

## The shelf

Every reader gets a shelf. Books on the go. Books up next. Books finished. Each one with its real cover, and a progress bar.

## Log a chapter

After a chapter, your child logs it in one tap. Olive, the reading buddy, asks a few friendly questions, one at a time. Kids type or talk. No quizzes, no scores.

## Streaks and badges

Reading every day grows a streak, and badges mark the firsts: the first book, the first week in a row. Small wins that keep kids coming back.

## Grown-ups

Grown-ups stay in the loop. Read the answers whenever it suits you, then send back a sticker and a little note. Kids keep every one.

## Rewards

When the last chapter is logged, Shelfie throws a party, and you give the reward you promised. Movie night, a trip to the bookshop, whatever your family loves.

## Outro

Shelfie. Every chapter, rewarded. Start reading free at shelfie dot kids.
