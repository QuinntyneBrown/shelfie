# 16 · Get notified

## Introduction

This video shows the little messages, called toasts, that Shelfie pops up after something happens. Good news comes and goes on its own. Problems stay put until someone deals with them.

## Toasts for success and errors

Nia logs chapter twelve of The Lion, the Witch and the Wardrobe and writes her first answer. Just as she presses I'm done, the connection hiccups. A danger toast says Couldn't save your answers, and tells her they are still on the page. It does not fade away, and her words stay safe in the box.

Once the connection is back, she presses Retry on the toast. This time it works. The page cheers Chapter twelve done, and a friendly toast says Chapter twelve saved, five to go, with Undo for ten seconds.

## Recap

Things to remember. Success toasts slip away by themselves, while error toasts stay until Nia presses Retry or dismisses them. If several pile up, only three show at once, with a more line that opens the rest.
