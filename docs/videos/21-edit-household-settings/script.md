# 21 · Edit household settings

## Introduction

Settings is where Dad looks after the household: who reads, how they sign in, and how Shelfie looks. Only the grown-up can open it.

## Changing Nia's colour and PIN

Dad opens his account menu and chooses Settings. The page shows the household as it is now: Nia as the reader, Dad as the grown-up, and his e-mail address.

He picks Mint as Nia's colour and types a new four-digit PIN. Leaving the PIN box blank would keep the old one. He presses Save settings, and a banner says Saved: Nia sees the changes the next time she signs in.

Dad chooses Switch reader and picks Nia. Her old PIN no longer works, and the keypad says so. With the new PIN her shelf opens, and the circle with her initial in the top bar is now mint.

## Recap

Things to remember. A new PIN must be exactly four digits. Changing the e-mail address or the password needs Dad's current password. And if anything is wrong, Shelfie says what to fix before it sends anything.
