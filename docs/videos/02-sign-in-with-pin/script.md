# 02 · Sign in with PIN

## Introduction

This is the second Shelfie journey: signing in with a PIN. Nia should get from the front door of the app to her books in a few taps, with no e-mail and no password to remember.

## Signing in with her PIN

Dad has signed in on this device once, so it already knows the household. Shelfie opens on a simple question: Who’s reading today? Nia taps her name, the screen says Hi Nia, and it asks her to tap her four-digit PIN.

Little fingers slip, so let us start with a mistake. She taps four nines. The dots empty again, and a friendly line says That PIN didn’t match, four tries left. Nothing scary, and nothing to clear by hand.

Now she taps one, two, three, four. That is right, so Shelfie takes her straight to her shelf, where it says Hi Nia and her books are waiting.

## Recap

Things to remember. Nia only ever needs her name and four digits. A wrong PIN clears the keypad and says how many tries are left. And after five wrong tries, the keypad takes a short break and locks for five minutes.
