# 04 · Add a book

## Introduction

This is the fourth Shelfie journey: adding a book. Before Nia can log a chapter, the book has to be on her shelf. Dad usually adds it, and it takes about a minute.

## Adding the book

Dad signs in with his e-mail and password, opens Books, and presses Add a book.

He types the title, The Hobbit, and the author, J. R. R. Tolkien. The author is optional. Then he counts the chapters in the contents page and enters nineteen.

Next comes the fun part. He picks a peach cover colour and a dragon sticker. The cover preview follows every change, so he sees the book before it lands on the shelf.

He presses Add to my shelf. A message says The Hobbit is on your shelf under Up next, and the next time Nia opens her shelf, it is waiting there with a button to start with chapter one.

## Recap

Things to remember. Only the title and the number of chapters are needed, and the chapters must be from one to two hundred. The preview shows the cover as you build it. And Nia can add a book herself too, from the Add a book button on her shelf.
