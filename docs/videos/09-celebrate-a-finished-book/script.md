# 09 · Celebrate a finished book

## Introduction

This is journey nine: celebrating a finished book. Finishing a whole book is a big deal, so Shelfie makes sure it feels like one.

## Finishing the book

Nia signs in with her PIN. The BFG has three chapters and she has already read two, so she presses the button for chapter three. She answers the first question and presses I'm done.

This time it is more than the usual little party. Confetti falls, a trophy appears, and the page says You finished the whole book! All three chapters of The BFG. Dad will pick her reward, so she should watch her shelf.

From here she can go straight to Add my next book, or head back to her shelf. She heads back, and The BFG has moved down to Finished, with a note that Dad is picking her reward. When she opens the book, a trophy card tells her the day she finished it.

## Recap

Things to remember. The celebration comes by itself with the last chapter. If Nia's device asks for reduced motion, she still gets the celebration, just without the confetti. And her reward shows up on her shelf once Dad gives it.
