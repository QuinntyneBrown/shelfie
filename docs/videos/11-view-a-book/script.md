# 11 · View a book

## Introduction

Every book on Nia's shelf has its own page. It shows how far she has got and what to read next.

## Inside a book

Nia taps The Lion, the Witch and the Wardrobe on her shelf. At the top, the progress bar says eleven of seventeen chapters read.

Below it is a list of every chapter. Each logged chapter shows the day she read it and the first line of her answer. A star and the words Dad read it mean Dad has already looked at it. Chapter eleven still says Waiting for Dad.

Chapter twelve is marked Up next, and the big I read Chapter twelve button takes her straight to logging it.

## Long and finished books

A long book like The Wild Robot has eighty chapters, so everything after chapter ten folds away and the page stays short. One tap opens the rest.

When a book is finished, like Charlotte's Web, the button is gone. In its place is a trophy and the reward Dad gave her: movie night with popcorn.

## Recap

Things to remember. Every logged chapter shows whether Dad has read it yet. The next chapter is always one tap away. And a finished book shows its reward.
