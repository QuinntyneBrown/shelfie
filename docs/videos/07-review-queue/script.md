# 07 · Review queue

## Introduction

This is journey seven: the review queue. Review is the first page Dad sees when he signs in, and it answers one question: what is waiting for me?

## The queue

Dad signs in and Review opens. The line under the title sums it all up: two chapters to read, and one reward to give. The Review link in the top bar carries the same count of chapters as a little badge.

This week shows how many chapters Nia read over the last seven days, and her reading streak.

Waiting for you lists the chapters newest first. Each one shows the book, the chapter, and the day Nia read it, with a Review button beside it.

Below the list, Matilda is finished, so its card offers Give a reward. And Recent activity tells the story in plain words, newest first.

## All caught up

When every chapter has been reviewed, the list makes way for a calm message instead. All caught up. Nothing is waiting, and Nia's next chapter will show up here.

## Recap

Things to remember. The newest chapter is always at the top. The badge on Review tells Dad at a glance how many are waiting. And finished books wait on the same page for their reward.
